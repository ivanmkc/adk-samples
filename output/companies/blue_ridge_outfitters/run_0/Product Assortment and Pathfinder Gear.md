# Blue Ridge Outfitters: Product Assortment and Pathfinder Gear

Blue Ridge Outfitters prides itself on offering a comprehensive selection of outdoor gear and apparel, catering to everyone from beginners to seasoned experts. This is achieved through a dual approach: stocking a wide array of products from leading industry brands alongside its own thoughtfully developed private label, Pathfinder Gear.

## Extensive Brand Selection

Across its retail stores and online platform, Blue Ridge Outfitters features equipment and apparel from over 150 renowned outdoor brands. Customers can find gear from industry giants such as Patagonia, Arc'teryx, The North Face, Black Diamond, Osprey, and Salomon, ensuring access to cutting-edge technology and trusted performance. The company manages a vast inventory, encompassing approximately 12,000 unique Stock Keeping Units (SKUs), covering diverse needs across various outdoor disciplines.

## Pathfinder Gear: Value and Performance

Complementing the selection of major brands is Blue Ridge Outfitters' private label, 'Pathfinder Gear'. Launched with a focus on providing durable, functional gear and apparel at a mid-range price point, Pathfinder Gear aims to deliver exceptional value, particularly for core outdoor activities like hiking and camping. This strategic initiative has proven successful, with the Pathfinder Gear label steadily growing since a major push in the early 2000s to currently account for approximately 18% of the company's total annual revenue.

## In-House Design and Rigorous Testing

Authenticity and quality are central to the Pathfinder Gear line. Products are designed in-house at the Blue Ridge Outfitters headquarters in Asheville, NC. A dedicated team, led by Marcus Davis (who also oversees product sourcing for the company), ensures that designs align with the company's heritage and meet the practical needs of outdoor enthusiasts.

Before reaching customers, new Pathfinder Gear designs undergo rigorous field testing. This crucial step is carried out by Blue Ridge Outfitters' own Guided Adventure Travel guides, who put the gear through its paces in diverse and demanding environments, ranging from the local Blue Ridge Mountains to international destinations like the Andes.

## Key Products and Sustainability

The Pathfinder Gear line includes essential equipment for various adventures. Key product categories feature technical backpacks (ranging from 20L daypacks to 80L+ expedition packs), lightweight tents suitable for backpacking and camping, synthetic and down sleeping bags rated for various temperatures, and both recreational and touring kayaks.

Several Pathfinder Gear items have become particularly popular among customers, including the 'Appalachian Trail' series of tents, the 'Blue Ridge' line of fleece jackets (notably made with recycled Polartec fleece), and the versatile 'Summit Seeker' backpacks.

Reflecting Blue Ridge Outfitters' commitment to environmental stewardship, recent Pathfinder Gear apparel collections increasingly incorporate sustainable materials and practices. This includes the use of recycled polyester fabrics and the adoption of PFC-free durable water repellent (DWR) finishes.

## Specialized Regional Assortments

Recognizing the specific needs of different outdoor communities, Blue Ridge Outfitters tailors its inventory in certain locations. The Denver, CO, and Jackson, WY stores, for instance, feature particularly robust winter sports assortments. This includes specialized backcountry safety gear essential for the surrounding mountain environments, such as avalanche beacons, probes, and shovels from respected brands like Mammut and Backcountry Access, alongside a wide selection of skis and splitboards.

Through its carefully curated mix of leading brands and the value-driven, thoughtfully designed Pathfinder Gear line, Blue Ridge Outfitters provides a comprehensive and reliable source for outdoor equipment and apparel.
