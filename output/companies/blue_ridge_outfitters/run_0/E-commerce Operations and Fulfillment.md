# Blue Ridge Outfitters: E-commerce Operations and Fulfillment

Blue Ridge Outfitters has cultivated a significant online presence through its website, blueridgeoutfitters.com, which complements its physical retail locations. Originally launched in 1999, the online store has evolved significantly and currently operates on a modern e-commerce platform. This platform enhances the customer experience with features like advanced product filtering, integrated customer reviews, and detailed technical specifications for outdoor gear, catering to the needs of informed enthusiasts. This online channel represents a substantial portion of the company's business, accounting for approximately 35% of total annual revenue.

## The Asheville Distribution Hub

Central to the company's online success is its dedicated distribution center (DC) located in Asheville, North Carolina. Constructed in 2005 adjacent to the corporate headquarters, this 30,000-square-foot facility serves as the primary fulfillment hub for nearly all orders placed through blueridgeoutfitters.com. Its establishment was a key step in supporting the growth initiated by the online store's launch.

## Warehouse Management and Staffing

Operations within the Asheville DC are highly organized, leveraging technology and a dedicated team. Around 2018, the company implemented a Warehouse Management System (WMS) to streamline processes. This system is crucial for maintaining inventory accuracy across the extensive range of approximately 12,000 unique Stock Keeping Units (SKUs) that Blue Ridge Outfitters offers. The WMS also optimizes order picking routes within the facility, improving efficiency.

A dedicated team of approximately 45 full-time staff manages the e-commerce operations exclusively. Their responsibilities cover the entire fulfillment lifecycle, including receiving incoming merchandise, inventory control and storage, order picking and packing, shipping, and processing customer returns.

## Order Processing and Shipping

Blue Ridge Outfitters maintains a standard service level agreement (SLA) for its online order processing. The goal is to have orders picked, packed, and shipped from the Asheville distribution center within 1-2 business days of receipt, ensuring timely delivery to customers.

To ship these orders, the company primarily utilizes established carriers, UPS and the United States Postal Service (USPS). Customers within the United States are typically offered standard ground shipping as well as expedited shipping options to meet varying delivery speed requirements.

## Customer Integration and Returns Management

Under the leadership of current CEO Amelia Davis, Blue Ridge Outfitters implemented a new Customer Relationship Management (CRM) system in 2021. This system integrates customer data gathered from blueridgeoutfitters.com with point-of-sale data from the flagship retail stores in Denver, Bend, and Jackson. This integration provides a unified view of customer purchase history across all channels, enabling more personalized service and marketing.

The Asheville distribution center is also equipped to handle all merchandise returns and exchanges initiated for purchases made through the website, providing a centralized point for managing the reverse logistics process.

## Analytics and Future Enhancements

The performance of blueridgeoutfitters.com is closely monitored by the marketing team, which was previously led by current CEO Amelia Davis. Key metrics such as conversion rate, average order value (AOV), and website traffic sources are tracked and analyzed. These insights inform digital marketing strategies and play a role in inventory planning for the Asheville DC.

Looking ahead, Blue Ridge Outfitters is focused on enhancing its e-commerce platform as part of its near-term strategy. Investments are being made in improving the mobile site's performance. Additionally, the company is exploring the potential addition of 'Buy Online, Pick Up In Store' (BOPIS) functionality, which might be piloted initially at the Asheville location, further bridging the online and physical retail experiences.
