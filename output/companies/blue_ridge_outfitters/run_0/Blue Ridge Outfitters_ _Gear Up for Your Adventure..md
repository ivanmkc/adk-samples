# Blue Ridge Outfitters: Gear Up for Your Adventure

For over half a century, Blue Ridge Outfitters has been equipping outdoor enthusiasts for their journeys. Founded more than 50 years ago by the avid mountaineer Jedediah 'Jed' Stone, the company has remained true to its roots. Headquartered in the vibrant city of Asheville, North Carolina, Blue Ridge Outfitters continues its legacy as a family-owned business, currently steered by the grandchildren of its founder.

## Operations and Reach

While firmly rooted in Asheville, Blue Ridge Outfitters extends its reach across the country. In addition to a comprehensive online store catering to customers nationwide, the company operates prominent flagship retail locations in key outdoor hubs: Denver, Colorado; Bend, Oregon; and Jackson, Wyoming. This physical presence allows customers to experience products firsthand and receive expert advice.

## Products and Expertise

Blue Ridge Outfitters offers a wide selection of gear and apparel tailored to various outdoor pursuits. The company specializes in equipment for hiking, camping, climbing, kayaking, and winter sports. Customers can find products from leading major outdoor brands alongside the company's own private label line, known as 'Pathfinder Gear', offering quality alternatives.

## Guided Adventures

Beyond retail, Blue Ridge Outfitters embraces the spirit of exploration by offering guided adventure travel services. These curated experiences cater to different levels of adventure, ranging from accessible local day hikes to challenging international trekking expeditions, allowing customers to fully immerse themselves in the outdoors under expert guidance.

## Values, Competition, and Future Vision

Commitment to the environment is a core value for Blue Ridge Outfitters. The company actively practices environmental stewardship by donating a percentage of its profits to support conservation efforts, ensuring the preservation of the wild places its customers explore.

In the competitive outdoor retail landscape, Blue Ridge Outfitters contends with major national retailers like REI and Dick's Sporting Goods. It also competes with specialized local outfitters in its store locations and various online brands.

Looking ahead, Blue Ridge Outfitters has ambitious plans to deepen its connection with the natural world by developing a network of branded eco-lodges situated near national parks, further enhancing the adventure experience for its clientele.
