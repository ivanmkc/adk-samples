# Pathfinder Gear: Product Lines and Market Performance

Pathfinder Gear, the private label brand of Blue Ridge Outfitters, has demonstrated significant growth and carved out a distinct niche within the outdoor gear market. Under the guidance of Marcus Davis, the brand's contribution to Blue Ridge Outfitters' total annual revenue has steadily increased, growing from approximately 12% five years ago to a current level of 18%. This reflects successful product development and marketing strategies focused on delivering value and performance.

## Core Product Offerings

Pathfinder Gear offers a range of equipment and apparel designed for core outdoor activities:

*   **Tents:** The 'Appalachian Trail' tent series is a notable example, primarily featuring 2-person and 3-person backpacking models. These tents are recognized for balancing weight (typically 4 to 6 pounds) with durability, offered at competitive price points between $200 and $300.
*   **Apparel:** The 'Blue Ridge' fleece line stands out as a consistent top-seller. Utilizing Polartec® 200 Series recycled fleece, these garments provide mid-weight insulation suitable for three-season use and have proven particularly popular in the Asheville market.
*   **Backpacks:** The 'Summit Seeker' backpack line caters to various trip lengths, with models ranging from 35-liter daypacks for local hikes to 65-liter multi-day packs for extended journeys. Key features include adjustable torso lengths and integrated rain covers.
*   **Accessories:** Beyond major gear items, the brand includes essential accessories like trekking poles made from lightweight 7075 aluminum, headlamps offering 300 to 500 lumens of brightness, and hydration reservoirs available in 2-liter and 3-liter capacities.

## Market Positioning and Pricing Strategy

Pathfinder Gear reinforces its mid-range value positioning through a strategic pricing approach. Products are typically priced approximately 25-35% below comparable technical items from premium brands like Arc'teryx or Patagonia that are also sold within Blue Ridge Outfitters. This strategy aims to attract customers seeking reliable performance without the highest price tag.

## Exclusive Distribution

Pathfinder Gear products maintain exclusivity through Blue Ridge Outfitters' own sales channels. They are available only at the company's four flagship retail stores (Asheville, Denver, Bend, Jackson) and through its e-commerce website, blueridgeoutfitters.com.

## Product Development and Rigorous Testing

New product designs undergo thorough evaluation. For instance, prototypes for new sleeping bags are often field-tested during Guided Adventure Travel division expeditions to demanding, high-altitude environments like the Everest Base Camp trek in Nepal or guided climbs in the Peruvian Andes. This ensures insulation performance meets real-world challenges.

Looking ahead, Pathfinder Gear is preparing to launch a new bikepacking collection targeted for Spring 2025. This line will include waterproof seat packs, handlebar rolls, and frame bags constructed from durable, high-denier, abrasion-resistant fabrics. These materials have undergone extensive testing for water ingress and attachment security in challenging conditions, such as those found in Pisgah National Forest.

## Commitment to Sustainability

Pathfinder Gear is actively working towards incorporating more sustainable materials. As of the end of 2023, approximately 40% of the brand's apparel line utilized Bluesign approved fabrics. This marks tangible progress towards the strategic goal of reaching 50% Bluesign approved fabrics by the end of 2026.
