# Second Generation Expansion (1995-2019)

The period from 1995 to 2019 marked a transformative era for Blue Ridge Outfitters, defined by significant expansion under the leadership of the founder's daughter. In 1995, Eleanor Stone-Davis took the helm as CEO, succeeding her father, Jedediah Stone, and initiating the company's second-generation leadership phase.

A pivotal early move under Eleanor Stone-Davis was the embrace of digital commerce. In 1999, the company launched its online store via blueridgeoutfitters.com. This strategic initiative dramatically expanded Blue Ridge Outfitters' reach beyond its physical retail footprint in Asheville, making its products accessible to a national customer base.

The success of the online store necessitated infrastructural growth. To effectively manage the increasing volume of online orders and inventory, the company constructed a dedicated 30,000-square-foot distribution center in 2005. This facility was strategically built adjacent to the Asheville headquarters, streamlining logistics and supporting the burgeoning e-commerce operations.

Parallel to digital growth, Eleanor Stone-Davis spearheaded the company's first major physical expansion outside of North Carolina. This westward expansion focused on establishing a presence in key outdoor recreation hubs in the Western United States.
*   **Denver, CO (2008):** The first flagship store outside the home state opened in Denver. This substantial 25,000-square-foot location served as a major anchor in the Rocky Mountain region.
*   **Bend, OR (2012):** The expansion continued with the opening of a flagship store in Bend, Oregon, tapping into the vibrant outdoor community of the Pacific Northwest.
*   **Jackson, WY (2016):** The final flagship location opened under Eleanor's leadership was in Jackson, Wyoming. This store featured specialized gear catering to the demanding backcountry activities prevalent in the nearby Teton Range.

During this era, particularly in the early 2000s, Blue Ridge Outfitters also significantly invested in developing its private label, 'Pathfinder Gear'. This strategic push aimed to offer quality, value-driven products designed in-house. The effort proved successful, with Pathfinder Gear growing to represent 18% of the company's total revenue by the end of this period.

Eleanor Stone-Davis concluded her 24-year tenure as CEO in 2019. Under her guidance, Blue Ridge Outfitters evolved from a single Asheville storefront into a dynamic multi-channel retailer with a robust online presence, a dedicated distribution center, and four flagship stores strategically positioned across the country, setting the stage for the third generation of leadership.
