# Blue Ridge Outfitters: Guided Adventure Travel Services

Blue Ridge Outfitters extends its passion for the outdoors beyond retail, offering a comprehensive range of Guided Adventure Travel services. This division, under the direction of Liam Stone, provides expertly led excursions ranging from local day trips near the Asheville headquarters to challenging international expeditions across the globe.

## Local Adventures Near Asheville

Based out of the Asheville, NC headquarters, the guided program offers accessible adventures in the surrounding Blue Ridge Mountains.
*   **Hiking:** Guided day hikes frequently explore scenic sections of the Appalachian Trail, including popular destinations like Max Patch and the Roan Highlands. These trips typically last between 4 and 6 hours.
*   **Water Sports:** During the summer months (May through September), adventurers can join guided kayaking and paddleboarding tours on the beautiful French Broad River.

## Domestic Winter Expeditions

Expanding beyond the Southeast, Blue Ridge Outfitters caters to winter sports enthusiasts near its western locations.
*   **Skiing and Snowshoeing:** From December through March, guided backcountry skiing and snowshoeing tours are available near the Denver, CO, and Jackson, WY stores. These trips often incorporate valuable avalanche safety training components.

## International Expeditions

Under Liam Stone's leadership, the Guided Adventure Travel division has significantly expanded its international offerings since 2019.
*   **Major Treks:** Signature trips include a challenging 14-day trek to Everest Base Camp in Nepal and a 10-day guided ascent of Mount Kilimanjaro in Tanzania.
*   **Expanded Destinations:** Newer additions feature multi-day treks in the stunning landscapes of Patagonia (Chile/Argentina) and guided hut-to-hut hiking tours through the Swiss Alps.
*   **Local Partnerships:** For international trips in destinations like Peru and Nepal, Blue Ridge Outfitters partners with carefully vetted and certified local guiding companies. This ensures smooth in-country logistics and provides authentic cultural experiences for participants.

## Commitment to Safety and Quality

Safety and client experience are paramount in all guided adventures.
*   **Guide Qualifications:** All lead guides for multi-day domestic and international trips are required to hold current Wilderness First Responder (WFR) certification. Furthermore, they must possess a minimum of five years of professional guiding experience.
*   **Guide-to-Client Ratios:** To ensure personalized attention and safety, groups maintain a strict guide-to-client ratio. For hiking and trekking groups, this ratio is 1:6 or lower. For more technical climbing programs, the maximum ratio is 1:2.

## Trip Pricing

The cost of guided trips varies significantly based on duration, location, and complexity.
*   **Local Day Trips:** Asheville-area day hikes typically range from $95 to $150 per person.
*   **International Expeditions:** Major international trips, such as the 14-day Everest Base Camp trek, start at approximately $4,500 per person, excluding the cost of international airfare.

## Role in Gear Development

The Guided Adventure Travel division serves a vital internal function by field-testing prototype gear from Blue Ridge Outfitters' private label, 'Pathfinder Gear'. Guides provide direct performance feedback from diverse real-world conditions to the product design team led by Marcus Davis, contributing to the development of high-quality equipment.
