# Blue Ridge Outfitters Eco-Lodge Network: Vision and Feasibility

Blue Ridge Outfitters is exploring a significant expansion of its brand experience through the development of a network of branded eco-lodges situated near national parks. This initiative represents a long-term strategic vision aimed at deepening customer engagement and reinforcing the company's commitment to sustainability and outdoor adventure.

## Vision and Sustainability Goals

Central to the eco-lodge concept is a strong commitment to environmental stewardship. Blue Ridge Outfitters is targeting a minimum LEED Gold certification for all planned lodges. This ambitious goal will be pursued through specific design and operational strategies, including:

*   Rainwater harvesting systems to conserve water resources.
*   Passive solar heating designs to minimize energy consumption.
*   Extensive use of locally sourced and sustainable building materials to reduce the project's carbon footprint and support regional economies.

## Feasibility Studies and Site Selection

The initial steps towards realizing this vision are currently underway. A dedicated internal task force, reporting directly to CEO Amelia Davis, is overseeing feasibility studies. These studies are initially focused on potential sites near two iconic locations: Great Smoky Mountains National Park and Grand Teton National Park, leveraging the company's existing presence in Asheville, NC, and Jackson, WY. Findings from these initial studies are expected by the fourth quarter of 2024.

Site selection is guided by rigorous criteria designed to ensure both exceptional guest experiences and minimal environmental impact. Key priorities include:

*   Parcels located adjacent to national park boundaries.
*   Direct access to trail networks for hiking and other activities.
*   Sites allowing for minimal environmental disruption during construction.
*   Favorable local zoning regulations for hospitality development.
*   Potential for year-round operation to maximize viability.

Comprehensive environmental impact assessments are integral to the feasibility stage. These assessments involve detailed studies on local wildlife corridors, potential impacts on water tables, and strategies for mitigating light pollution to protect the sensitive ecosystems near park boundaries.

## The Pilot Eco-Lodge Project

Blue Ridge Outfitters envisions launching a pilot eco-lodge between 2027 and 2028. Tentative plans for this first lodge include:

*   Approximately 20 guest rooms.
*   A communal gathering space designed to reflect the aesthetic of Blue Ridge Outfitters' flagship retail stores.
*   An on-site classroom facility for hosting workshops, such as Leave No Trace principles training.

A clear timeline guides the pilot project: site acquisition is targeted for finalization by mid-2025, followed by the completion of architectural design and LEED planning by early 2026. Construction is projected to commence by late 2026.

## Operational Structure and Funding

Operationally, the eco-lodges are planned to be managed under a newly established 'Blue Ridge Hospitality' division. This will necessitate recruiting staff with specific expertise in sustainable lodging management and high-quality guest services.

Funding for the pilot project is anticipated to come from a combination of sources. Blue Ridge Outfitters plans to utilize retained earnings generated from its retail and e-commerce growth, while also potentially seeking specialized green financing options or partnering with impact investors who align with the company's sustainability mission.

## Integration with Core Offerings

The eco-lodges are designed to seamlessly integrate with Blue Ridge Outfitters' core retail and adventure travel businesses. Planned amenities include dedicated gear storage and drying rooms for guests engaging in outdoor activities. Furthermore, guests will have the convenience of on-site booking capabilities for guided trips and activities offered through the Guided Adventure Travel division, directed by Liam Stone.

Package deals are envisioned to enhance the guest experience, bundling lodging with exclusive guided day hikes or skills clinics led by Blue Ridge Outfitters guides. Examples include fly fishing clinics near the Great Smoky Mountains location or wildlife photography workshops near Grand Teton.

This carefully planned eco-lodge initiative underscores Blue Ridge Outfitters' commitment to sustainable growth and providing immersive outdoor experiences for its customers. The phased approach, beginning with thorough feasibility studies and a pilot project, reflects a strategic commitment to responsible development aligned with the company's long-standing values.
