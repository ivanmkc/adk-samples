## Blue Ridge Outfitters: Asheville Headquarters and Logistics Hub

The nerve center of Blue Ridge Outfitters' operations is firmly rooted in Asheville, North Carolina. Located at 1 Appalachian Way, the company's headquarters complex serves as both its corporate home and a critical logistics base, supporting its retail stores, online presence, and guided adventures.

### Corporate Headquarters at 1 Appalachian Way

The main headquarters building encompasses 50,000 square feet and houses the essential corporate functions that drive the company forward. Key departments operating from this location include Finance, Human Resources, IT support, and Marketing, which was previously led by current CEO Amelia Davis. It also serves as the administrative hub for the Guided Adventure Travel division, directed by Liam Stone. Approximately 120 corporate and administrative staff work within this building.

Designed to support collaboration and innovation, the corporate office space features dedicated design studios utilized by the Pathfinder Gear team, led by Marcus Davis. Multiple conference rooms are equipped for video conferencing, facilitating communication with the flagship stores in Denver, Bend, and Jackson. Employees also benefit from a break area offering views of the scenic Blue Ridge Mountains.

### Integrated Warehouse and Design Space

Directly adjacent to the corporate offices, within the same 50,000 square foot structure, lies crucial warehouse space. This area primarily functions as storage for raw materials and prototype samples for the Pathfinder Gear line. It also houses a small workshop used by Marcus Davis's design team for hands-on product development. Furthermore, a dedicated section within this warehouse stores and maintains gear and equipment essential for guided day trips originating near Asheville, such as kayaks for French Broad River tours and demo gear used for Appalachian Trail hikes.

### E-commerce Distribution Center

Supporting the significant online business generated through blueridgeoutfitters.com, a separate 30,000 square foot e-commerce distribution center stands next to the headquarters. Constructed in 2005 to handle the growing demands of online sales, this facility is technologically equipped with a Warehouse Management System (WMS) integrated with handheld RF scanners. This system, implemented around 2018, allows for real-time inventory tracking across the approximately 12,000 Stock Keeping Units (SKUs) managed by the company.

This distribution center operates with a dedicated staff of approximately 45 employees focused solely on e-commerce fulfillment. During the peak holiday season, typically November through December, the center ramps up operations significantly, processing an average of 800 to 1,000 online orders per day.

As part of Blue Ridge Outfitters' commitment to sustainability and its goal of achieving a 25% waste reduction by 2025, the roof of the distribution center was retrofitted in 2022 with a 100 kW solar panel array to offset a portion of its electricity consumption.

### Seamless Coordination

Effective logistical coordination between the corporate headquarters and the adjacent distribution center is vital. This is achieved through shared inventory visibility provided by the WMS and regular cross-departmental meetings involving operations, marketing, and merchandising teams, ensuring smooth operations from product design to final customer delivery.

### Regional Impact

Combined, the Blue Ridge Outfitters headquarters and the e-commerce distribution center represent a significant presence within the outdoor industry sector in the Asheville metropolitan area, serving as a major employer and the operational core for the entire company.
