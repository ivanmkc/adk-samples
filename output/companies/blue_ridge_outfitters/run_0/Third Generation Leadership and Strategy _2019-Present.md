# Third Generation Leadership and Strategy (2019-Present)

The year 2019 marked a significant transition for Blue Ridge Outfitters as the third generation of the founding Stone family assumed key leadership roles, continuing the company's legacy while charting a new course for the future. Led by CEO Amelia Davis, with her brother Marcus Davis overseeing the Pathfinder Gear private label and cousin Liam Stone directing the Guided Adventure Travel division, this new era focuses on digital innovation, enhanced sustainability, product evolution, and strategic growth in adventure travel.

## Strategic Direction and Digital Advancement under Amelia Davis

Since stepping into the CEO role in 2019, Amelia Davis has prioritized modernizing the company's operations and customer engagement. A key initiative was enhancing digital capabilities, culminating in the implementation of a new Customer Relationship Management (CRM) system in 2021. This system aimed to better integrate customer data across online and retail channels, providing a more seamless experience.

The early tenure of the new leadership was quickly tested by the global COVID-19 pandemic in 2020. Under Amelia Davis's direction, the company responded rapidly by implementing enhanced safety protocols across its flagship locations (Denver, Bend, Jackson, Asheville). Recognizing shifting consumer needs, they also expanded virtual gear consultations and introduced curbside pickup services to maintain customer service and safety.

Sustainability remains a core strategic focus under Amelia Davis. Building on the company's existing commitments, she has overseen efforts towards achieving measurable targets, including the goal of diverting 25% of operational waste from landfills by 2025, using 2020 as the baseline year.

## Pathfinder Gear: Sustainability and Performance under Marcus Davis

Marcus Davis has steered the Pathfinder Gear private label towards greater sustainability and performance since 2019. A notable step was the introduction of the first collection featuring Bluesign-approved fabrics in Fall 2021, showcased in the updated 'Blue Ridge' fleece jacket series. This aligns with the ambitious company goal of utilizing 75% recycled or renewable materials in Pathfinder Gear apparel by 2028.

Furthering this commitment, Marcus initiated a partnership in 2023 with Polartec to incorporate their innovative Power Air™ fabric into upcoming apparel designs. This fabric is specifically known for reducing microfiber shedding, addressing a key environmental concern associated with synthetic textiles.

Beyond materials, Marcus Davis also formalized and expanded the Pathfinder Gear field testing program after 2019. This involved incorporating structured feedback from sponsored regional athletes based near the Denver and Bend stores, supplementing the valuable insights already gathered from the company's own adventure travel guides.

## Guided Adventure Travel: Expansion and Responsibility under Liam Stone

Liam Stone has focused on both expanding the reach and enhancing the responsibility of the Guided Adventure Travel division since taking the helm in 2019. Demonstrating a commitment to international growth, he spearheaded the launch of a new 12-day guided trek in Chile's Torres del Paine National Park in 2022.

Domestically, the division capitalized on increased demand for US-based outdoor experiences, achieving a notable 25% increase in bookings for multi-day trips between 2021 and 2023.

Alongside expansion, Liam Stone emphasized responsible travel practices. In 2020, he mandated enhanced cultural sensitivity training and advanced Leave No Trace ethics modules for all guides leading international trips, with a particular focus on expeditions operating in sensitive environments like Nepal and Peru.

## Celebrating Milestones

The third-generation leadership also oversaw the company's 50th Anniversary celebration in 2022. This milestone was marked by various initiatives, including a large community gathering at the Asheville headquarters. The event featured exhibits from conservation partners and workshops led by Pathfinder Gear designers, reinforcing the company's connection to its community and heritage.

Under its current leadership, Blue Ridge Outfitters continues to navigate the evolving retail landscape, balancing its family-owned heritage with forward-looking strategies centered on digital integration, sustainable practices, product innovation, and curated adventure experiences.
