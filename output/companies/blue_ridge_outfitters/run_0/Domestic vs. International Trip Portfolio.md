# Blue Ridge Outfitters: Domestic vs. International Trip Portfolio

Blue Ridge Outfitters, through its Guided Adventure Travel division led by Liam Stone, offers a diverse and extensive portfolio of guided trips. Annually, the division curates and operates approximately 40 distinct itineraries, catering to a wide range of interests and skill levels, from accessible single-day activities near its retail hubs to challenging multi-week expeditions across the globe.

## Domestic Adventures: Exploring America's Backcountry

Leveraging its presence in key outdoor recreation areas, Blue Ridge Outfitters provides numerous guided experiences within the United States.

*   **Near Asheville, NC:** The company's headquarters serves as a base for local adventures. Guided day hikes frequently explore scenic sections of the nearby Appalachian Trail, such as Max Patch or Roan Highlands, typically lasting 4-6 hours. During the summer months (May-September), guided kayaking and paddleboarding tours are offered on the French Broad River, primarily utilizing stable recreational sit-on-top kayaks suitable for beginners.
*   **Near Denver, CO:** Capitalizing on proximity to the Rocky Mountains, offerings include a 5-day guided backpacking trip through Colorado's Indian Peaks Wilderness, usually available from July to September. These trips incorporate practical skills training, covering map and compass navigation, Leave No Trace principles, campsite selection, and backcountry cooking techniques using provided group gear. Winter programs focus on safety, featuring certified AIARE Level 1 Avalanche Courses (December-February) that combine classroom instruction with field sessions. Guided backcountry skiing and snowshoeing tours are also available during winter months.
*   **Near Jackson, WY:** Similar to Denver, the Jackson location supports winter safety programs, including AIARE Level 1 courses and guided backcountry skiing/snowshoeing from December through March, often integrating avalanche safety training relevant to the Teton environment.

## International Expeditions: Global Trekking and Mountaineering

Blue Ridge Outfitters extends its guiding expertise to iconic international destinations, often partnering with certified local operators.

*   **European Alps:** The portfolio features a classic 7-day guided trek along the Haute Route, traversing from Chamonix, France, to Zermatt, Switzerland. This hut-to-hut journey offers stunning alpine scenery and utilizes traditional mountain huts for accommodation.
*   **South America:** In collaboration with local partners in Peru, the company offers a 9-day guided trek on the Inca Trail to the historic sanctuary of Machu Picchu. This trip includes necessary permits and crucial acclimatization days in Cusco. Further south, multi-day treks in Patagonia (Chile/Argentina) have been added to the offerings.
*   **African Summits:** A popular expedition is the 10-day guided ascent of Mount Kilimanjaro in Tanzania. The trip typically follows the Machame Route, chosen for its scenic beauty and favorable acclimatization profile.
*   **Himalayan Treks:** The division organizes a 14-day trek to Everest Base Camp in Nepal. The itinerary includes strategically planned acclimatization days in key locations like Namche Bazaar (approx. 11,286 ft) and Dingboche (approx. 14,470 ft) to mitigate altitude sickness risks.

## Logistics, Support, and Safety Standards

Blue Ridge Outfitters ensures a high standard of service and safety across all its guided adventures.

*   **Booking:** All guided trips, from local day hikes to major international expeditions, are managed through a dedicated section on the blueridgeoutfitters.com website and supported by specialized Adventure Travel Consultants based at the Asheville headquarters.
*   **Pre-Trip Support:** Participants booked on major international expeditions like Kilimanjaro or Everest Base Camp receive comprehensive pre-trip support packages. These include detailed gear checklists, physical training recommendations, visa and vaccination information, and access to pre-trip Q&A sessions with lead guides.
*   **Guide Expertise:** All lead guides for multi-day domestic and international trips are required to hold current Wilderness First Responder (WFR) certification and possess a minimum of 5 years of professional guiding experience.
*   **Safety Ratios:** To ensure personalized attention and safety, a standard guide-to-client ratio of 1:6 or lower is maintained for trekking trips. For example, a typical Everest Base Camp group consists of 10-12 clients accompanied by 2 lead guides and local support staff managed through partner agencies.
*   **Pricing:** Trip costs vary significantly based on duration, location, and complexity. Local Asheville-area day hikes typically range from $95-$150 per person, while major international expeditions like the 14-day Everest Base Camp trek start around $4,500, and the 10-day Kilimanjaro climb starts around $5,000 (both excluding international airfare).

The Guided Adventure Travel division not only provides memorable experiences but also serves a vital role in field-testing new 'Pathfinder Gear' products, ensuring the company's private label gear meets the demands of real-world conditions encountered on these diverse domestic and international journeys.
