# Founding Vision and Early Growth (1972-1995)

Blue Ridge Outfitters began its journey in 1972, founded by the avid mountaineer Jedediah 'Jed' Stone. His vision for the company stemmed directly from his personal experiences hiking sections of the Appalachian Trail near Asheville, North Carolina, shaping the initial direction and ethos of the brand.

The very first Blue Ridge Outfitters retail store opened its doors that same year, 1972, in Asheville. Located on Biltmore Avenue, this original establishment was modest, occupying just 800 square feet. Initially, the store's product offerings were carefully curated to serve the specific needs of hikers and campers exploring the surrounding Blue Ridge Mountains region.

Jedediah Stone personally guided and managed the company throughout this entire foundational period, from its inception in 1972 until 1995. His leadership during this founding era spanned a total of 23 years. This period represented the company's single-location phase, with all focus centered exclusively on the Asheville store before any geographic expansion occurred.

A significant milestone during this early growth phase was the establishment of the company's first major conservation partnership in 1985. Demonstrating an early commitment to local environmental stewardship, Blue Ridge Outfitters chose the Southern Appalachian Highlands Conservancy as its inaugural partner, reflecting the company's roots in the region's natural landscape.
