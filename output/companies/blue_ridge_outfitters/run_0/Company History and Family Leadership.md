# Blue Ridge Outfitters: A Family Legacy Through Generations

## Founding and Early Growth

The story of Blue Ridge Outfitters began in 1972, born from the passion of its founder, Jedediah Stone. Inspired by his own experiences hiking the nearby Appalachian Trail, Jedediah established the company to serve fellow outdoor enthusiasts. The first Blue Ridge Outfitters store was a modest beginning, occupying an 800-square-foot space on Biltmore Avenue in Asheville, North Carolina, which remains the company's headquarters city.

Jedediah Stone led the company during its formative years, establishing its reputation and commitment to the outdoors. This commitment extended beyond retail early on; in 1985, Blue Ridge Outfitters established its first major conservation partnership with the Southern Appalachian Highlands Conservancy, demonstrating a dedication to environmental stewardship that continues today.

## Second Generation Expansion

Jedediah ran the company for over two decades before passing the torch to the next generation. In 1995, his daughter, Eleanor Stone-Davis, took over as CEO. Eleanor's leadership marked a period of significant growth and adaptation for Blue Ridge Outfitters. Recognizing the changing retail landscape, she spearheaded the launch of the company's online store in 1999, greatly expanding its reach. Under her guidance, the company also began its physical expansion beyond Asheville, opening the Denver, Colorado flagship location in 2008.

## Third Generation at the Helm

The tradition of family leadership continued into the 21st century. In 2019, the transition to the third generation occurred, with Jedediah Stone's grandchildren stepping into key leadership roles, ensuring the company remained a family-owned business.

Today, the company is led by Amelia Davis, Jedediah's eldest grandchild, who serves as CEO. Amelia brought prior experience from within the company, having previously managed the marketing department. Her brother, Marcus Davis, plays a crucial role in product development, overseeing the company's private label line, 'Pathfinder Gear', and managing product sourcing. Their cousin, Liam Stone (son of Jedediah's other child), directs the Guided Adventure Travel division. Liam has been instrumental in expanding the scope of these services, adding international trekking expeditions to destinations like the Alps and Andes to the existing offerings.

## Milestones and Continued Commitment

In 2022, Blue Ridge Outfitters celebrated a significant milestone: its 50th anniversary. The occasion was marked with special events and the launch of a commemorative 'Heritage Collection' under the successful Pathfinder Gear label, honoring the company's long history. From its humble beginnings on Biltmore Avenue to its current status with multiple retail locations, a thriving online presence, and diverse offerings, Blue Ridge Outfitters continues its journey under the stewardship of the founder's family.
