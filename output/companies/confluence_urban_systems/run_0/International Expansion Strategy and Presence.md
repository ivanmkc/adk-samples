# Confluence Urban Systems: International Expansion Strategy and Presence

Confluence Urban Systems actively pursues international expansion, extending its mission to create more livable, resilient, and equitable urban environments beyond its North American base. The company employs a targeted strategy focused on collaborating with cities committed to climate action and leveraging local partnerships to deliver tailored smart city solutions.

## Strategic Targeting: Climate Leadership Networks

A cornerstone of Confluence's international strategy involves actively targeting cities within prominent global networks like the C40 Cities Climate Leadership Group and the Global Covenant of Mayors for Climate & Energy. The company leverages the publicly stated climate action plans and resilience goals of these member cities as strategic entry points. International project proposals developed by Confluence explicitly detail how the proposed technological solutions align with the specific climate commitments previously declared by the target city through its participation in these networks. This approach ensures that Confluence's offerings directly address the documented priorities of potential municipal partners.

## Current International Footprint (as of Early 2024)

As of early 2024, Confluence maintains active project engagements or consultancy roles in at least three international cities outside the United States:

### Asia-Pacific Hub: Singapore

The Singapore office, officially opened in the third quarter of 2021, serves as Confluence's primary hub for business development and project delivery across the Asia-Pacific (APAC) region. This office employs a dedicated team of approximately eight staff members. This team includes regional business development managers and technical consultants who specialize in adapting Confluence solutions for the unique challenges of tropical climates and high-density urban environments common throughout Southeast Asia.

### European Engagement: Copenhagen, Denmark

Confluence has established a presence in Europe, notably through an air quality sensor deployment project in Copenhagen initiated in 2022. The selection of Copenhagen was directly influenced by its prominent role in the Global Covenant of Mayors for Climate & Energy and its ambitious municipal goals for achieving carbon neutrality by 2025. For all European projects, including the work in Copenhagen, Confluence ensures its data platforms and citizen-facing applications are fully compliant with the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

### North American Presence: Vancouver, Canada

Confluence's successful entry into Vancouver, Canada, demonstrates the effectiveness of its strategic targeting. The city's active C40 membership and its pre-existing 'Smart Vancouver' initiative created a receptive policy environment for Confluence's integrated smart city solutions. This alignment facilitated the adoption of Confluence technologies and services, including the 'MyVancouver Connect' application.

## Expansion Strategy, Partnerships, and Objectives

Looking ahead, Confluence's international strategy includes plans to evaluate potential locations for new regional hubs by the end of 2026. Cities under consideration include Amsterdam or Berlin for a European hub, and Medellin or Santiago for a Latin American hub.

A key component of successful international expansion involves forming strategic partnerships. Confluence actively seeks collaborations with local system integrators and established technology providers within target countries. These partnerships are crucial for navigating deployment logistics effectively and ensuring compliance with national regulations and standards.

Furthermore, Confluence has set specific strategic objectives for its international growth. A primary goal is to secure contracts for at least two large-scale, multi-year Public-Private Partnership (PPP) projects outside North America by the end of 2025, solidifying its role as a global partner in urban resilience and smart city development.
