# Core Biological Interventions: Gene Editing and Cellular Rejuvenation

BioHarmonix employs sophisticated biological interventions centered around precision gene editing and comprehensive cellular rejuvenation protocols, tailored to individual client needs. These programs, GeneWeave Precision Editing and ChronoGuard Cellular Therapy, represent the core of the company's approach to optimizing healthspan and mitigating disease risk.

## GeneWeave Precision Editing

The GeneWeave platform is BioHarmonix's flagship gene-editing service, designed for high-precision somatic cell modifications.

*   **Core Technology:** The system primarily utilizes **Cas-BHX-Omega**, a proprietary Cas enzyme variant. This enzyme is derived from Dr. Li's Nobel-winning Cas-HF1 but has undergone further optimization through AI-driven protein engineering. This process enhances its specificity against human somatic targets, achieving remarkably low off-target modification rates, reported below 0.001% in internal validation studies.
*   **Targeted Applications:**
    *   **Hereditary Cancer Risk Reduction:** For clients with high-risk **BRCA1 or BRCA2** mutations, GeneWeave protocols aim to correct the mutation within targeted somatic stem cell populations, such as mammary stem cells. A full course of therapy typically achieves a 70-85% reduction in the client's calculated lifetime risk for associated cancers.
    *   **Neurodegenerative Disease Predisposition:** The platform targets the **APOE4** allele associated with late-onset Alzheimer's disease. This involves editing hematopoietic stem cells *ex vivo* before reinfusion, aiming to convert the E4 variant to the more neutral E3 variant. Internal data suggests a potential risk reduction of 40-60% for individuals homozygous for the E4 allele.
    *   **Metabolic Disorders:** GeneWeave has been successfully applied in select clients to correct somatic mutations in the **PCSK9** gene, which are associated with severe familial hypercholesterolemia. This intervention has led to significant reductions in LDL cholesterol levels that were previously refractory to conventional treatments.

## ChronoGuard Cellular Therapy

The ChronoGuard program is a multi-faceted approach to cellular rejuvenation, combining stem cell therapy, partial reprogramming, senolytics, and exosome treatments.

*   **Autologous Mesenchymal Stem Cells (MSCs):** MSCs are harvested from the client's own adipose tissue, typically via lipoaspiration during the initial Baseline Assessment Program. These cells are then expanded over 3-4 weeks using proprietary growth media formulations developed at the La Jolla facility before being administered.
*   **Partial Reprogramming:** Modified mRNA encoding specific **Yamanaka factors** (Oct4, Sox2, Klf4, c-Myc, often supplemented with Lin28 and Nanog depending on the client's epigenetic profile) is used. These mRNAs are encapsulated in lipid nanoparticles (LNPs) engineered for transient expression and targeted delivery. Administration occurs intravenously in pulsed cycles, typically for 3 days each month.
*   **Senolytic Therapy:** BioHarmonix utilizes its lead senolytic compound, **BHX-SenoClear**. Preclinical models show high efficacy against senescent vascular endothelial cells expressing the p16INK4a marker. Phase 0 human microdosing studies indicate that optimal systemic clearance is achieved via intravenous infusion cycles administered quarterly.
*   **Exosome Infusions:** The program incorporates exosomes purified from the client's own rejuvenated MSC cultures. Purification involves tangential flow filtration (TFF) followed by size-exclusion chromatography, ensuring high purity and concentration. These vesicles deliver beneficial microRNAs and growth factors like VEGF and TGF-β1.
    *   **Quality Control:** Exosome preparations undergo rigorous quality control, including Nanoparticle Tracking Analysis (NTA) to confirm size distribution (typically 50-150 nm) and concentration, and western blot analysis to verify key exosomal markers (CD9, CD63, TSG101) and rule out cellular contaminants.
*   **Integrated Annual Cycle:** A standard ChronoGuard cycle for systemic rejuvenation typically involves:
    *   Four quarterly infusions of expanded autologous MSCs.
    *   Monthly pulsed LNP-mRNA Yamanaka factor treatments.
    *   Bi-monthly infusions of BHX-SenoClear.
    The specific schedule and dosages are continuously adjusted based on biomarker feedback analyzed by the Aura Health AI platform.
