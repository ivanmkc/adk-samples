# Elite Client Vetting and Onboarding at BioHarmonix

Access to BioHarmonix's advanced services is predicated on an exceptionally rigorous vetting and onboarding process, designed to ensure financial capacity, philosophical alignment, and security compatibility with the organization's exclusive environment. This multi-stage procedure reflects the company's commitment to discretion and its high-stakes mission.

## Initial Vetting Stages

**Financial Verification:**
Prospective clients must meet stringent financial criteria. The formal requirement is a minimum verifiable net worth of $100 million USD. Furthermore, candidates must demonstrate at least $25 million in documented liquid assets. This financial standing is subject to meticulous forensic accounting verification conducted by contracted Tier-1 accounting firms during the vetting phase.

**Comprehensive Background Checks:**
BioHarmonix engages specialist security firms, such as Kroll, to perform background checks that significantly exceed standard financial and legal due diligence. These investigations delve into a candidate's digital footprint, analyze known associates, assess potential geopolitical exposure, and scrutinize any historical factors that could present a security or reputational risk to BioHarmonix or its existing clientele.

**Philosophical Alignment Assessment:**
Candidates undergo an assessment to ensure their philosophical views align with BioHarmonix's ethos. This involves a standardized, in-house developed psychometric evaluation known as the 'Thorne-Li Congruence Index'. Additionally, multiple interviews are conducted with senior BioHarmonix staff, including an ethicist, to probe the candidate's perspectives on radical life extension, human enhancement, and their acceptance of potentially experimental protocols.

**Nomination Process:**
While nomination by an existing BioHarmonix client serves as a strong recommendation, it does not guarantee acceptance. The nominating client must submit a formal letter attesting to the candidate's character and discretion. Despite the nomination, the nominee is still required to successfully pass all independent vetting stages.

## Process Timeline and Selectivity

The standard pre-assessment vetting timeline, from the point of initial contact or nomination to receiving approval for the subsequent Baseline Assessment Program, typically averages four months. This period involves coordinated efforts between BioHarmonix's internal security and legal teams, as well as external vetting firms. BioHarmonix maintains a stringent rejection rate. Internal data indicates that fewer than 30% of individuals who commence the vetting process, including those nominated, are ultimately accepted into the full program following the Baseline Assessment. Failures most commonly occur during the rigorous background, financial, or philosophical checks.

## The Baseline Assessment Program (BAP) and Onboarding

Candidates who successfully navigate the initial vetting stages may be approved for the intensive two-week Baseline Assessment Program (BAP) held at the La Jolla facility.

**Program Fee:**
Participation in the BAP requires a non-refundable fee of $1.5 million. This fee must be paid in full via secure wire transfer at least 30 days prior to the client's arrival. It covers all diagnostics, initial consultations, security arrangements, and luxury accommodations provided during the two-week assessment period.

**Integration and Training:**
During the BAP, clients engage in daily integration sessions with their assigned Client Liaison Director (CLD) team. These sessions establish secure communication protocols using the proprietary BioHarmonix Connect application and allow for the customization of concierge preferences within the secure facility. As a mandatory part of onboarding, clients receive personalized training on operational security (OPSEC) best practices. This includes instruction on secure communication methods via the BioHarmonix Connect app, specific travel protocols, and guidelines for maintaining strict confidentiality regarding their affiliation with BioHarmonix.

## Final Approval

Successful completion of the Baseline Assessment Program does not automatically guarantee full acceptance. It culminates in a final review conducted by the BioHarmonix Admissions Committee. This committee is co-chaired by the Head of Client Relations and the Chief Medical Officer. Only after their approval is the comprehensive, multi-hundred-page Master Service Agreement formally offered to the client, signifying their full admission into the BioHarmonix program.
