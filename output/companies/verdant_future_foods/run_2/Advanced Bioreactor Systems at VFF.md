# Advanced Bioreactor Systems at Verdant Future Foods

Verdant Future Foods (VFF) leverages sophisticated bioreactor technology as the cornerstone of its cellular agriculture operations, enabling the production of its innovative cultured meat products like CryoSteak and Ocean's Bounty. These advanced systems are meticulously designed and controlled to cultivate high densities of cells under optimal conditions.

## Perfusion Technology and Filtration

At the heart of VFF's cultivation strategy are perfusion bioreactors. These systems primarily employ Tangential Flow Filtration (TFF) utilizing hollow fiber cartridges. This setup allows for the continuous removal of metabolic waste products, such as lactate and ammonia, which can inhibit cell growth at high concentrations. Simultaneously, the TFF system retains the valuable cells within the bioreactor while facilitating the constant replenishment of VFF's proprietary serum-free growth medium, ensuring a steady supply of nutrients for the demanding cultivation process.

## Scalability: From Pilot to Commercial Production

VFF operates bioreactors at various scales to support both research and production needs. The current pilot-scale facility in Emeryville, California, houses stainless steel bioreactors with working volumes ranging from 50 liters up to 500 liters. Looking towards commercialization, VFF plans significant expansion. The projected facility in Singapore is set to feature multiple stainless steel bioreactors, each with a capacity of 10,000 liters or potentially larger, designed to meet commercial-scale production demands.

## Sophisticated Control and Monitoring

A complex Supervisory Control and Data Acquisition (SCADA) system serves as the central nervous system for managing VFF's bioreactor fleet. This system integrates real-time data streams from a suite of sophisticated sensors embedded within the bioreactors. These include Raman spectroscopy probes, pH sensors, dissolved oxygen (DO) sensors, temperature probes, and pressure transducers. The SCADA system uses this continuous flow of information to maintain precise environmental setpoints crucial for optimal cell growth and product consistency.

## Real-Time Process Analytical Technology: Raman Spectroscopy

A key component of VFF's monitoring strategy is the use of integrated Raman spectroscopy probes. These provide non-invasive, real-time measurements of critical metabolite concentrations directly within the culture medium, including glucose, lactate, glutamine, and glutamate. Data is typically acquired every 15 to 30 minutes and fed directly into the SCADA system. This enables automated adjustments to nutrient feed rates, ensuring cells receive exactly what they need when they need it. The accuracy of this system is maintained through calibration using multivariate chemometric models developed in-house by VFF, which correlate the spectral data with offline measurements obtained from instruments like a BioProfile FLEX2 analyzer. This ensures the real-time monitoring is specifically tuned for VFF's unique cell lines and media formulations.

## Maintaining Optimal Growth Conditions

**Sterilization:** Ensuring aseptic conditions is paramount, especially given the long cultivation cycles (typically 3-4 weeks) required for products like CryoSteak. VFF utilizes validated Steam-in-Place (SIP) sterilization protocols for its stainless steel pilot-scale bioreactors before each batch run.

**Environmental Control:** Precise control over dissolved oxygen (DO) and pH is critical for cell viability and metabolism. DO levels are typically maintained between 40-60% saturation through the automated sparging of oxygen and nitrogen gas mixtures. The pH is tightly controlled around the physiological range of 7.2-7.4 via the automated addition of CO2 gas or a sodium bicarbonate solution, managed by the SCADA system based on real-time sensor feedback.

**Agitation:** To ensure adequate mixing for nutrient distribution and gas transfer without causing excessive shear stress that could damage cells, VFF employs specialized low-shear agitation systems. Depending on the scale and specific cell type, these may include custom-designed marine impellers or gentle rocking platforms, optimized for performance in the high-viscosity serum-free media used.

## Specialized High-Pressure Systems

Producing certain products, like the 'Ocean's Bounty' line featuring marine cells, requires unique environmental conditions. VFF utilizes specialized, pressure-rated stainless steel bioreactors capable of operating at high pressures (10-15 bar) to mimic deep-sea environments. These ASME-certified vessels are equipped with reinforced seals and dedicated pressure control loops fully integrated into the SCADA system, ensuring safe and precise operation under demanding conditions.

## Data Management and Compliance

The vast amount of data generated by the bioreactor sensors, including Raman spectra and environmental parameters (pH, DO, temperature, pressure), is systematically archived. VFF uses a secure, cloud-based Laboratory Information Management System (LIMS) for this purpose. This data repository is crucial for ongoing process optimization efforts, compiling detailed batch records, and generating documentation required for regulatory compliance.

In summary, VFF's advanced bioreactor systems represent a highly integrated and controlled environment, combining sophisticated hardware, real-time analytics, and robust data management. This technological infrastructure is fundamental to the company's ability to reliably produce high-density cell cultures for its pioneering cultured meat products.
