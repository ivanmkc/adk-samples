# Integrated Farm and Creamery Operations at Heartland Family Farms

Heartland Family Farms Creamery exemplifies a deeply integrated approach, seamlessly blending dairy farming with artisanal cheese production. This model allows for meticulous control over quality, from pasture to finished product.

## The Farm Foundation: Herd and Pasture Management

The foundation of Heartland's operation is its dedicated dairy farm. The primary herd consists of approximately 150 cows, with a focus on Holstein and Jersey breeds. These breeds are specifically chosen for the high butterfat content of their milk, a crucial characteristic for producing the rich, flavorful cheeses and butter the creamery is known for.

Sustainable practices are central to farm management. The farm utilizes rotational grazing across its extensive 500+ acres. This method not only provides the cows with nutrient-rich pastures, contributing to milk quality, but also actively promotes soil health and environmental stewardship.

## Milk Sourcing: Quality and Freshness

While the farm's own herd provides a significant portion of the milk, Heartland Family Farms also collaborates with its local community. To meet production demands, the creamery sources supplemental milk from five carefully selected neighboring family farms. Maintaining freshness is paramount, so all partner farms are located within a 20-mile radius of the creamery, ensuring milk arrives quickly for processing.

## Artisanal Creamery Production

The creamery serves as the heart of the cheese-making operation. Annually, it processes over 2 million pounds of milk. This raw ingredient is skillfully transformed into approximately 200,000 pounds of finished artisanal cheese each year.

A key element in developing their signature flavors is the aging process. Heartland's award-winning aged cheddars mature in a dedicated cellar, built in 1995. This facility features precisely controlled temperature and humidity, allowing cheeses to develop complex characteristics over periods ranging from 12 months to over 5 years. Beyond cheddars, the creamery showcases creativity with unique offerings like the popular seasonal 'Ramp Havarti'. This cheese incorporates wild ramps foraged from nearby woodlands and is typically available only during a short window in late spring, usually April and May.

## Commitment to Sustainability

Heartland's commitment to sustainability extends beyond grazing practices. In 2018, the farm took a significant step towards renewable energy by installing a 50kW solar panel array on the main creamery building. This installation now supplies approximately 30% of the facility's annual electricity needs, reducing its environmental footprint.

## Business Operations and Community Engagement

Integrating the public and contributing to the local economy are also vital aspects of Heartland's operations. The visitor center, established in 2005 as part of a diversification strategy, has become a popular destination, attracting over 15,000 visitors annually for farm tours and cheese tastings.

The on-site retail store is another successful component, generating about 25% of the creamery's total revenue. The consistent best-selling cheese variety in the store is the 2-year aged 'Heritage Cheddar'. Leadership also plays an active role in the wider industry; CEO Sarah Miller currently serves on the board of the Wisconsin Cheese Makers Association, contributing to the state's renowned cheese community.

Through careful management of its herd, land, partnerships, and production processes, Heartland Family Farms demonstrates the success of an integrated farm and creamery model, rooted in quality, sustainability, and community connection.
