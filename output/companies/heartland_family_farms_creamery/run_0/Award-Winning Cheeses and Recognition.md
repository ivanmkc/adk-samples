# Award-Winning Cheeses and Recognition at Heartland Family Farms Creamery

Heartland Family Farms Creamery's dedication to producing high-quality, artisanal dairy products has earned significant recognition through prestigious awards and media features, highlighting their standing in the competitive world of cheese and dairy.

## Prestigious Cheese Competition Wins

A standout achievement came in 2021 when the creamery's 2-year 'Heritage Cheddar' was awarded the coveted Blue Ribbon at the Wisconsin State Fair Cheese Contest. This top honor was specifically earned in the 'Aged Cheddar (1-2 years)' category. Head Cheesemaker Maria Sanchez proudly accepted the award on behalf of the creamery during the ceremony held in West Allis, Wisconsin.

Further demonstrating their expertise, Heartland's Smoked Gouda, known for its distinct flavor imparted by smoking over hickory wood chips, received a Silver Medal at the highly respected American Cheese Society (ACS) Judging & Competition in 2019. This award was granted in the 'Smoked Cheeses - Cow's Milk' category.

## Recognition Beyond Cheese

The creamery's excellence extends beyond cheese. Their European-style Cultured Butter, notable for its high 84% butterfat content, achieved a 'Top 3 Finish' in its category at the World Dairy Expo Championship Dairy Product Contest held in Madison, Wisconsin, in 2020.

Heartland Family Farms Creamery demonstrates an ongoing commitment to quality assessment and improvement by regularly submitting entries to both the Wisconsin State Fair Cheese Contest and the American Cheese Society Judging & Competition. Typically, they enter 3-5 different cheese varieties each year into these competitive events.

## Celebrating Success and Impact

The physical symbols of these achievements, specifically the 2021 Blue Ribbon for the Heritage Cheddar and the 2019 ACS Silver Medal for the Smoked Gouda, are proudly displayed in a dedicated trophy case within the on-site retail store for visitors to see.

The recognition has translated into tangible business success. Following the 2021 Blue Ribbon win, wholesale orders for the award-winning 'Heritage Cheddar' experienced a noticeable increase of approximately 15% over the subsequent six months, underscoring the value of such accolades.

## Media Spotlight

Heartland Family Farms Creamery has also captured the attention of food media. A framed copy of a feature article from the renowned 'Culture: The Word on Cheese' magazine, published in 2022, is prominently displayed within the visitor center's dedicated tasting room.

In 2023, the creamery was featured on the regional television program 'Wisconsin Foodie'. The episode highlighted artisanal food producers located in Green Valley County, showcasing Heartland's contribution to the local food scene.

Local recognition followed the state fair success as well. The 'Green Valley Gazette', the local newspaper, celebrated the 'Heritage Cheddar's' Blue Ribbon win with a front-page article in August 2021, which included interviews with CEO Sarah Miller.

These awards and features serve as testaments to the skill, dedication, and quality inherent in the products crafted at Heartland Family Farms Creamery.
