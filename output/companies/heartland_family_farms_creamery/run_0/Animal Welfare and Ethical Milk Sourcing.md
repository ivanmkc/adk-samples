# Commitment to Animal Welfare and Ethical Milk Sourcing at Heartland Family Farms

Heartland Family Farms Creamery places a strong emphasis on animal welfare and ethical sourcing practices, recognizing them as fundamental to their identity and the quality of their artisanal dairy products. This commitment extends from their own herd to their valued local partner farms.

## High Standards for Animal Care

Heartland adheres to specific, stringent animal welfare standards across its operations and requires the same from its partner dairies. Key aspects include:

*   **Pasture Access:** Cows enjoy significant access to pasture, grazing for a minimum of 8 hours per day during the typical Wisconsin grazing season (May through October). Partner farms must provide readily accessible shaded areas and fresh water sources in all pasture paddocks utilized during this time. Heartland promotes the link between this pasture-based grazing and enhanced milk quality, citing internal analysis suggesting higher levels of beneficial fatty acids like Omega-3s and Conjugated Linoleic Acid (CLA), which contribute to the unique flavor profile of their cheeses.
*   **Housing and Comfort:** Minimum bedding standards are enforced in housing areas to ensure cow comfort. Adherence to specific stocking density guidelines, as outlined in Heartland's supplier contracts, prevents overcrowding.
*   **Herd Health:** A proactive herd health program focuses on preventative care. This includes documented protocols for lameness prevention and treatment. Heartland actively monitors herd health, aiming to maintain a lameness score below 10% through routine locomotion scoring conducted quarterly by trained staff and the partner veterinarian from Green Valley Veterinary Clinic.

## rBST-Free Guarantee

Heartland Family Farms guarantees that milk from both its own herd and its five partner farms is free from artificial growth hormones like recombinant bovine somatotropin (rBST). Verification of this guarantee is rigorous, involving signed affidavits submitted quarterly by each partner farm and random milk sample testing for prohibited hormones conducted twice annually by an independent laboratory.

## Ethical Partnerships with Local Farms

Heartland sources supplemental milk from five carefully selected neighboring family farms, all located within a 20-mile radius. The relationship with these partners is built on mutual respect and shared values:

*   **Premium Pricing:** Recognizing the commitment required to meet higher welfare standards, Heartland pays its partner dairies a consistent premium averaging $1.50 per hundredweight (cwt) above the prevailing Federal Milk Marketing Order Class III price.
*   **Long-Term Stability:** Long-term supply agreements, typically spanning 3-year terms, provide partner farms with guaranteed market access and price stability. This fosters mutual investment in sustainable practices and supports the economic viability of these local family operations.

## Verification and Training

Commitment to these standards is ensured through ongoing verification and training:

*   **Annual Site Visits:** Comprehensive annual verification site visits are conducted at each partner farm. These involve a detailed checklist review covering animal health records, facility conditions (cleanliness, ventilation, bedding), observation of animal handling practices, and confirmation of pasture access logs.
*   **Employee Training:** All Heartland farm employees involved in animal care undergo mandatory annual training. This training is based on the National Dairy FARM (Farmers Assuring Responsible Management) Program guidelines, covering essential topics like stockmanship, proper handling techniques, and recognizing signs of animal distress.

## Transparency with Consumers

Heartland believes in transparency regarding its sourcing and animal welfare practices. This commitment is communicated through:

*   **Website Information:** A dedicated 'Our Promise' section on their website details their ethical sourcing and animal welfare commitments.
*   **Product Packaging:** Specific call-outs on product packaging, such as 'Pasture-Raised' and 'rBST-Free Guarantee', highlight these key attributes directly to consumers.

By integrating high animal welfare standards, ethical sourcing partnerships, and transparent communication, Heartland Family Farms Creamery ensures that its commitment to quality begins with the care and respect provided to the animals and the local farming community.
