# Heartland Family Farms Creamery: Distribution Channels and Sales Mix

Heartland Family Farms Creamery utilizes a diversified strategy to bring its artisanal dairy products from its Green Valley County farm to consumers. This multi-channel approach balances regional wholesale partnerships, direct-to-consumer sales through an on-site store and e-commerce platform, and seasonal market participation.

## Regional Wholesale Distribution

The largest portion of Heartland's business flows through its regional wholesale channel, accounting for approximately 60% of total sales volume. Distribution focuses primarily on southern and central Wisconsin. To ensure product freshness and quality, Heartland operates a dedicated refrigerated van, making weekly deliveries to its network of roughly 45 regional wholesale accounts, primarily specialty food retailers.

The top-selling products within this channel are the 1-year Aged Cheddar and the Creamy Gouda. These are typically supplied in 8-ounce pre-packaged cuts, a format favored by retail partners. A key partner contributing significantly to this volume is the "Wisconsin Provisions Co-op" based in Madison, which represents nearly 10% of Heartland's total wholesale business. To foster strong relationships and support sales, Heartland provides its wholesale partners with point-of-sale materials, such as branded shelf talkers detailing cheese characteristics and pairing suggestions, and offers staff training sessions for key accounts.

## On-Site Retail Store

The creamery's on-site retail store in Green Valley County is a vital direct-to-consumer channel, averaging 25% of total annual revenue. This channel experiences a significant seasonal peak during the summer tourist season (June-August), where its contribution can climb to nearly 35% of monthly revenue. This surge is driven by the high volume of visitors participating in farm tours and tastings, with the visitor center attracting over 15,000 people annually.

The on-site store is the exclusive outlet for certain products due to logistical constraints. Notably, Heartland's popular ice cream pints, including flavors like 'Pasture Perfect Vanilla Bean' and seasonal offerings such as 'Honey Lavender', are only available here, as the cold chain requirements prevent distribution through wholesale or e-commerce channels. The store also serves as a primary point of sale for other items, including limited-release cheeses.

## E-commerce Sales

Heartland's direct online sales channel, facilitated through its website, accounts for approximately 15% of sales volume. This channel sees its highest traffic during the Q4 holiday season (November-December). Curated gift boxes featuring multiple cheese varieties are particularly popular during this period, making up over 40% of online sales.

The average online order value is around $75. This is often influenced by customers purchasing multiple cheese varieties and butter to meet thresholds for flat-rate or free shipping, indicating the effectiveness of shipping incentives. The e-commerce platform allows Heartland to reach customers nationwide, utilizing insulated packaging to maintain product integrity during transit.

## Farmers' Market Presence

Seasonally, from May through October, Heartland participates in the Dane County Farmers' Market in Madison. While contributing approximately 5% of total annual revenue, this presence serves as an important direct sales outlet, particularly for unique seasonal cheeses like the spring Ramp Havarti, connecting the farm directly with consumers in a vibrant market setting.

## Special Product Distribution

The distribution of highly limited products, such as the 5-year reserve cheddar, is tightly managed. These special releases are primarily sold through the on-site retail store and the e-commerce platform. Less than 5% of the annual batch is allocated to a select few high-end restaurant partners in Milwaukee and Madison, ensuring exclusivity.

In summary, Heartland Family Farms Creamery employs a strategic mix of distribution channels tailored to different products, customer segments, and geographical reaches, effectively balancing broad regional wholesale with targeted direct-to-consumer engagement.
