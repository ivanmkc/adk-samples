# Heartland Family Farms Creamery: A Portfolio of Artisanal Excellence and Recognition

Heartland Family Farms Creamery distinguishes itself through a diverse and carefully crafted portfolio of artisanal dairy products, reflecting a deep commitment to quality and tradition. Sourcing milk primarily from their own pasture-raised herd and select neighboring farms, the creamery transforms this high-quality ingredient into award-winning cheeses, rich butter, and unique ice creams.

## Diverse Cheese Offerings

The cornerstone of Heartland's production is its cheese. The creamery maintains a core portfolio of approximately 12 year-round cheese varieties. This selection is dynamically supplemented by 3-4 rotating seasonal cheeses, allowing for creative expressions using timely ingredients, such as the popular spring 'Ramp Havarti', which incorporates wild ramps foraged from nearby woodlands and is typically available only in late spring (April-May).

### Award-Winning Cheddars
Heartland's cheddars are a testament to their expertise in aging. The celebrated 'Heritage Cheddar', aged for 2 years, earned significant recognition with a Blue Ribbon at the Wisconsin State Fair Cheese Contest in 2021. Beyond this award-winner, the creamery offers a range of cheddar aging profiles to suit different palates, including varieties aged for 1 year, 3 years, and even a limited-release 5-year reserve cheddar for those seeking exceptional sharpness and complexity. These cheddars mature in a dedicated cellar with controlled temperature and humidity.

### Distinctive Goudas and Fresh Cheeses
The creamery's Gouda selection showcases both traditional and innovative approaches. Their popular Smoked Gouda is naturally smoked over hickory wood chips for a full 8 hours, imparting a deep, savory flavor. The standard Creamy Gouda is aged for a minimum of 60 days, developing a smooth texture and mild taste, while special reserve batches may age up to 6 months for enhanced sharpness. A notable seasonal favorite is the 'Winter Spice Gouda', infused with warming notes of cinnamon, nutmeg, and cloves, typically available from November through January.

Complementing the aged cheeses, Heartland produces fresh mozzarella daily. Sold in 8-ounce balls, its freshness makes it a highly sought-after item at the on-site retail store, often selling out by the afternoon.

## Beyond Cheese: Butter and Ice Cream

Heartland extends its artisanal craft to other dairy staples. They produce a European-style cultured butter boasting an 84% butterfat content, prized for its richness and flavor, available in both salted and unsalted 8-ounce packages.

Exclusive to their retail store, the creamery offers delightful ice cream flavors in pint containers. Popular choices include 'Pasture Perfect Vanilla Bean' and 'Chocolate Holstein Fudge', providing a sweet taste of the farm's high-quality milk.

## Recognition and Reputation

The quality of Heartland Family Farms Creamery's products has not gone unnoticed. The Blue Ribbon for their 'Heritage Cheddar' at the Wisconsin State Fair is a significant accolade. Furthermore, the creamery's dedication to farmstead cheese-making and quality was highlighted in a 2022 feature in 'Culture: The Word on Cheese' magazine, bringing national attention to their process and products. This recognition underscores their position as a respected producer of fine artisanal dairy goods.
