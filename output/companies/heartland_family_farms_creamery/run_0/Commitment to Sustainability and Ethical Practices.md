# Commitment to Sustainability and Ethical Practices at Heartland Family Farms

Heartland Family Farms Creamery operates with a deep-rooted commitment to sustainable farming practices and ethical operations, viewing stewardship of the land, responsible animal care, and community support as integral to its identity and success. This commitment is woven through every aspect of the farm and creamery, from pasture management to partner relationships.

## Animal Welfare and Responsible Milk Sourcing

The well-being of the dairy herd is paramount. The farm's approximately 150 Holstein and Jersey cows benefit significantly from rotational grazing. During Wisconsin's typical grazing season (May through October), these cows have access to fresh pasture for at least 8 hours daily across the farm's extensive acreage. This practice not only contributes to animal health but also enhances soil vitality.

Heartland guarantees that all milk used in its products, whether from its own herd or its partner farms, is free from artificial growth hormones like recombinant bovine somatotropin (rBST). This commitment extends to its sourcing partners. Heartland works closely with five local family farms, all situated within a 20-mile radius. To ensure consistency in quality and ethical standards, these partner farms are required to meet specific animal welfare and milk quality benchmarks, which are verified through annual site visits by Heartland management.

## Environmental Stewardship

A comprehensive approach to environmental protection guides farm operations. A detailed Nutrient Management Plan is strictly followed, utilizing composted manure from the dairy herd to fertilize the 500+ acres dedicated to growing feed. This practice significantly reduces the reliance on synthetic fertilizers and actively protects the watershed of the nearby Green Valley Creek.

Water conservation is actively pursued through measures like high-efficiency plate coolers, which pre-cool milk using less energy and water, and the implementation of water-saving cleaning protocols within the creamery. Waste reduction is another key focus. Nearly all of the whey produced during cheese making, estimated at over 1.8 million pounds annually, is repurposed. The majority is supplied to local farms as a nutritious liquid feed supplement for pigs, turning a potential waste stream into a valuable resource.

The farm actively promotes biodiversity across its land. Buffer zones planted with native grasses and wildflowers are maintained along waterways and field edges, creating essential habitats for pollinators and beneficial insects. Furthering their conservation efforts, Heartland participates in initiatives with the Green Valley County Land & Water Conservation Department, employing practices such as cover cropping on over 100 acres each fall to prevent soil erosion and enhance soil health. Complementing these land-based efforts, a 50kW solar panel array installed on the creamery roof supplies approximately 30% of the facility's annual electricity needs.

## Transparency and Ethical Partnerships

Heartland believes in transparency and actively shares its practices with the public. Farm tours, attracting over 15,000 visitors annually, serve as an educational platform, showcasing the rotational grazing system, the solar panel array, and discussing animal care protocols openly.

This ethical approach extends to business relationships. Heartland fosters long-term agreements with its partner dairy farms, paying a consistent premium over conventional milk prices. This practice supports the economic sustainability of these neighboring family operations, reinforcing the interconnectedness of the local agricultural community.

Through these integrated practices—from pasture to production, waste reduction to water conservation, and animal care to community support—Heartland Family Farms Creamery demonstrates a profound and ongoing commitment to operating sustainably and ethically.
