# Future Growth and Innovation Initiatives at Heartland Family Farms Creamery

Heartland Family Farms Creamery, building on its rich history and commitment to quality, is actively pursuing several strategic initiatives aimed at future growth, innovation, and enhanced sustainability. These plans encompass expanding organic offerings, developing exciting new products, improving direct-to-consumer sales channels, deepening environmental stewardship, and enriching the visitor experience.

## Organic Expansion Goals

A significant focus for the coming years is the expansion into certified organic production. Heartland aims to achieve organic certification for at least 25% of its milk supply within the next five years. This initiative will initially focus on launching certified organic versions of two of its most popular products: the best-selling Heritage Cheddar and the European-style Cultured Butter. To facilitate this transition, the creamery plans to provide technical assistance and potential financial incentives to encourage its five partner dairy farms, all located within a 20-mile radius, to adopt certified organic practices.

## New Product Development Pipeline

Innovation in cheese-making remains a core priority. The creamery's research and development team is actively working on developing a distinctive cave-aged blue cheese, targeting a market launch within the next two years. This project may involve collaborations with Green Valley County breweries to explore unique beer-washed rind variations. Furthermore, Heartland is exploring partnerships with local sheep dairies to source milk for a potential new line of sheep's milk cheeses, with initial development possibly focusing on a Pecorino-style variety.

## Enhancing E-commerce and Direct Sales

Recognizing the growing importance of direct customer relationships, Heartland is investing in its e-commerce platform. A key project is the planned launch of a customizable cheese subscription box service by the end of 2024. This initiative, along with other enhancements, aims to achieve a 30% increase in direct online sales revenue within three years. Future upgrades to the e-commerce website will concentrate on improving the mobile user experience and integrating innovative "virtual tasting kits" paired with guided video content.

## Deepening Sustainability Commitments

Building upon existing efforts like the 50kW solar array, Heartland is exploring further advancements in sustainability. A feasibility study is currently underway for installing an anaerobic digester system by 2027. This system would convert manure from the farm's 150-cow herd into biogas, which could be used for heating and potentially electricity generation. Additionally, the creamery is actively researching and testing new packaging solutions, with the goal of transitioning at least 50% of its product packaging to fully compostable or recycled materials by 2028.

## Expanding the Visitor Experience

To further connect with consumers and showcase its farmstead operation, Heartland is considering enhancements to its visitor offerings. A preliminary market study is underway for a potential on-site farm-to-table bistro, tentatively named 'The Creamery Table'. Located near the visitor center, it would feature a menu heavily focused on Heartland's cheeses and other local Wisconsin products. Additionally, to offer a more immersive educational opportunity, the creamery plans to introduce small-group, hands-on cheese-making workshops, potentially starting in 2025, providing a deeper dive than the current tours and tastings.

These forward-looking initiatives demonstrate Heartland Family Farms Creamery's dedication to evolving its offerings, strengthening its connection with customers, and reinforcing its commitment to sustainable practices for the future.
