# Heartland Family Farms Creamery Embarks on New Cheese Development Initiatives

Building on its reputation for artisanal quality and farmstead authenticity, Heartland Family Farms Creamery is actively pursuing several exciting new cheese development initiatives. These projects reflect the creamery's commitment to innovation and expanding its diverse portfolio of dairy products, driven by a dedicated research and development effort.

## Research and Development Framework

Spearheading these efforts is the R&D team, led by Head Cheesemaker Maria Sanchez and supported by a dedicated food science technician. This team focuses specifically on new variety development, running approximately 10 to 15 small, 50-pound experimental cheese batches each year. Progress is carefully monitored through monthly internal sensory evaluation panels. These panels, composed of creamery management, cheesemakers, and retail staff, assess experimental batches, including prototypes for the new blue and sheep's milk cheeses, providing crucial feedback to guide refinement. Market research, including customer surveys distributed via the 'Heartland Happenings' email newsletter and at the visitor center, further informs development priorities, indicating strong consumer interest, particularly in a cave-aged blue cheese.

## Cave-Aged Blue Cheese Project

A major focus is the development of a distinctive cave-aged blue cheese. The goal is to achieve a specific balance of pungency and creaminess, reminiscent of classic French Roquefort but expressing a unique Wisconsin terroir. This involves experimenting with different strains of *Penicillium roqueforti* cultures.

To support this initiative, Heartland is exploring modifications to a section of its existing 1995 affinage cellar. Plans include enhanced humidity controls and specific isolation measures. These changes are crucial for creating the ideal blue cheese aging environment while mitigating the risk of blue mold spores cross-contaminating the creamery's established cheddar aging program within the same cellar. Based on ongoing test batch evaluations and allowing for sufficient aging time, the target launch window for this new blue cheese is tentatively set for the holiday season of 2025.

## Sheep's Milk Cheese Initiative

Heartland is also venturing into sheep's milk cheeses. The initial focus is on developing a Pecorino-style cheese. The target profile is a firm, slightly granular cheese suitable for grating, aged for a minimum of eight months to develop nutty and slightly piquant flavors.

To secure a source for high-quality sheep milk, Heartland has initiated contact with Shepherd's Knoll Dairy, a small, family-run sheep farm located about 30 miles away in adjacent Oak Ridge County. Discussions are underway to assess the feasibility and consistency of their milk supply. A key challenge identified early on is the potential seasonality of sheep milk production, as local dairies like Shepherd's Knoll often have seasonal lactation cycles. This might necessitate launching the sheep's milk cheese initially as a seasonal offering.

## Beer-Washed Rind Exploration

In addition to the blue and sheep's milk projects, the creamery is in the early stages of exploring a beer-washed rind cheese. Initial discussions have involved the Green Valley Brewing Co., a local craft brewery. Consideration is being given to using their robust Porter to wash the cheese rind during aging, aiming to impart unique coffee and chocolate notes to the final product.

Through these dedicated initiatives, Heartland Family Farms Creamery continues to innovate, leveraging its expertise, local partnerships, and customer feedback to craft unique and high-quality additions to the Wisconsin artisanal cheese landscape.
