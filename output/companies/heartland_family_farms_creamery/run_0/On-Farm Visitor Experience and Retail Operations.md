# On-Farm Visitor Experience and Retail Operations at Heartland Family Farms Creamery

Heartland Family Farms Creamery offers more than just artisanal dairy products; it provides an immersive on-farm experience centered around its visitor center and retail operations. This direct engagement with the public is a cornerstone of the creamery's identity, allowing visitors to connect with the source of their food and contributing significantly to the business's success.

## The Visitor Center Hub

Opened to the public in June 2005, the 4,000 square foot visitor center serves as the main hub for public interaction. It welcomes over 15,000 visitors annually, offering insights into the farm's operations. The peak season for visits aligns with Wisconsin's primary tourism months, running from June through October. During this five-month period, the farm attracts approximately 60% of its yearly visitors, totaling around 9,000 people.

## Guided Farm Tours

A highlight for many visitors is the standard farm and creamery tour. Lasting approximately 60 minutes and costing $10 per adult (children under 12 are free), these guided tours offer a comprehensive look at the operation. Visitors can view the milking parlor (during scheduled times), observe the cheese-making process through dedicated windows, and see the cheese aging cellar. The tours actively showcase Heartland's commitment to sustainability, highlighting practices like rotational grazing, the farm's 50kW solar panel array, and animal care protocols.

Tours are primarily led by a team of 4 trained, part-time guides during the peak season (May-October), often recruited from local university agricultural programs or the community. Due to their popularity, securing a spot requires booking online through the creamery's website. Reservations often fill up one to two weeks in advance, especially for weekend slots during the busy months of July and August.

## Intimate Cheese Tastings

For those looking to delve deeper into the flavors of Heartland, cheese tastings are available. These sessions cost $15 per person and are held in a dedicated tasting room within the visitor center, designed to comfortably accommodate groups of up to 20 participants. Tastings typically feature a flight of 4-5 different cheeses, showcasing fresh, aged, and seasonal varieties when available. The experience is enhanced with local crackers and guided commentary from staff.

## The On-Site Retail Store

A significant portion of the visitor center, approximately 1,500 square feet, is dedicated to the on-site retail store. This space is crucial, generating about 25% of the creamery's total revenue. It operates year-round with a core staff of 3 full-time employees responsible for sales, stocking, and customer service. During the busiest visitor months from June to October, this team is supplemented by 2 additional part-time staff members.

## Retail Highlights and Local Goods

The consistent best-selling cheese variety in the retail store is the 2-year aged 'Heritage Cheddar'. Annual sales of this single product through the on-site store alone are estimated at approximately 15,000 pounds, making it a major driver of store revenue. Following the 'Heritage Cheddar', the next highest revenue-generating products sold primarily through the store are the 8-ounce packages of 84% butterfat European-style Cultured Butter and the pint containers of 'Pasture Perfect Vanilla Bean' ice cream.

Beyond Heartland's own dairy products, the store offers branded merchandise such as t-shirts and cheese boards. It also features a curated selection of complementary local Wisconsin products, including honey, maple syrup, and artisanal crackers. These items collectively account for approximately 10% of the store's total annual revenue.

## Special Seasonal Events

The visitor center also serves as a venue for special seasonal events throughout the year. Examples include Spring Cheese & Pairing Workshops. A notable annual event is the Holiday Open House, typically held on the first weekend of December. This festive gathering regularly attracts between 200 and 300 attendees over its two-day duration, offering special product bundles and a celebratory atmosphere.

In conclusion, the visitor center, tours, tastings, retail store, and special events create a multifaceted on-farm experience that is integral to Heartland Family Farms Creamery's connection with its customers and its overall business model.
