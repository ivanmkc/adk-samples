# Seasonal and Specialty Cheese Varieties at Heartland Family Farms Creamery

Beyond its acclaimed core portfolio of approximately 12 year-round cheese varieties, Heartland Family Farms Creamery delights customers with a rotating selection of unique seasonal and specialty cheeses. These limited-edition offerings showcase local ingredients, innovative flavor combinations, and the creative expertise of the creamery's cheesemakers, providing a distinct taste experience often tied to the rhythms of the Wisconsin seasons.

## Celebrating the Seasons with Unique Flavors

Heartland's commitment to using local ingredients shines through in its seasonal cheese program, which typically features 3-4 rotating varieties throughout the year. These cheeses often incorporate ingredients available only during specific times, making their release highly anticipated.

### Spring Delight: Ramp Havarti

A standout spring offering is the 'Ramp Havarti'. This popular seasonal cheese features wild ramps, known for their pungent, garlicky-onion flavor. Demonstrating a commitment to sustainability, the ramps are harvested by a licensed forager from designated areas within the nearby Kettle Moraine State Forest during their short growing window, typically in late April. This cheese captures the essence of Wisconsin spring and is often featured prominently in the 'Spring Cheese & Pairing Workshops' hosted at the visitor center, where it's paired with local rhubarb preserves and artisanal rye crackers from a Madison bakery. Availability is fleeting, usually limited to April and May.

### Winter Warmth: Spice Gouda

As the weather turns colder, Heartland introduces its 'Winter Spice Gouda', typically available from November through January. This comforting cheese is infused with a proprietary blend of warming spices, including Korintje cinnamon, Indonesian nutmeg, and Madagascar cloves. These high-quality spices are sourced through a specialty purveyor located in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, adding a festive and aromatic dimension to the creamy Gouda base.

### Past Seasonal Innovations

The creamery occasionally introduces other limited-run seasonal cheeses. For example, in 2019, Heartland offered a 'Maple Bourbon Cheddar' as a fall seasonal. This unique cheese involved infusing their 1-year aged cheddar with Wisconsin maple syrup and then briefly aging it in barrels sourced from Great Northern Distilling in Plover, Wisconsin, showcasing creative collaborations and flavor profiles.

## Specialty Craftsmanship: Hickory Smoked Gouda

While perhaps available more consistently than strictly seasonal offerings, Heartland's Hickory Smoked Gouda stands out as a specialty variety. This popular cheese undergoes a traditional cold-smoking process. Using locally sourced Wisconsin hickory wood chips, the cheese is smoked for 8 hours in a dedicated smokehouse chamber. Typically smoked in 5-pound loaves before being cut for retail, this process imparts a deep, authentic smoky flavor that complements the creamy Gouda.

## From Concept to Counter: Development and Production

The creation of new seasonal and specialty cheeses is spearheaded by Head Cheesemaker Maria Sanchez. She leads the development process, often experimenting with small test batches of around 50 pounds for 6 to 12 months. This careful experimentation allows for refinement of recipes before committing to larger seasonal production runs.

Production for seasonal cheeses like the Ramp Havarti and Winter Spice Gouda is intentionally limited, typically capped at approximately 1,500 pounds per season. This small-batch approach ensures quality and exclusivity, often resulting in the cheeses selling out at the farm store and online within just 4 to 6 weeks of their release.

## Pricing, Availability, and Presentation

Reflecting the unique ingredient costs, smaller batch sizes, and specialized labor involved, seasonal and specialty cheeses like the Ramp Havarti and Winter Spice Gouda are typically priced about 15-20% higher per pound than Heartland's standard Creamy Gouda or 1-year Aged Cheddar.

Due to their limited production volume, these special cheeses are primarily sold directly to consumers through Heartland's on-site retail store and the creamery's e-commerce website. Only minimal amounts are allocated to select wholesale partners in Madison and Milwaukee, making them a sought-after find for those visiting the farm or shopping online.

To further highlight their special nature, seasonal cheeses feature unique label designs distinct from the core range. Created by a local Green Valley County graphic designer, these labels incorporate seasonal motifs – such as spring graphics for the Ramp Havarti or winter themes for the Spice Gouda – visually signaling their limited availability and connection to the time of year.

## A Taste of the Moment

Heartland Family Farms Creamery's seasonal and specialty cheeses offer a delicious way to experience the changing seasons and the innovative spirit of Wisconsin artisanal cheesemaking. From the fleeting taste of wild ramps in spring to the warm spices of winter, these limited-edition creations provide unique flavor experiences eagerly awaited by cheese enthusiasts.
