## Artisanal Butter and Ice Cream Production at Heartland Family Farms Creamery

Beyond its renowned artisanal cheeses, Heartland Family Farms Creamery dedicates significant craft to producing high-quality, small-batch butter and ice cream, utilizing the rich milk from its own pasture-raised Holstein and Jersey cows. These offerings, primarily available at the on-site retail store, embody the same commitment to quality ingredients and traditional methods found in their cheesemaking.

### European-Style Cultured Butter

Heartland produces a distinctive European-style cultured butter known for its high butterfat content (84%) and characteristic tangy flavor. This unique taste profile is developed through a careful fermentation process where fresh cream is cultured with specific strains of *Lactococcus lactis* for 12 to 16 hours before churning.

The butter is crafted using a 50-gallon stainless steel batch churn, with each cycle yielding approximately 200 pounds of finished product. Annually, the creamery produces around 5,000 pounds of this sought-after butter. It is packaged exclusively in 8-ounce foil-wrapped blocks, available in both salted and unsalted varieties, and sold directly at the farm's retail store. The unsalted version has gained particular favor among artisanal bakeries in nearby Madison, Wisconsin, who make special trips to procure it for creating high-quality croissants and other pastries requiring premium butter.

### Small-Batch Artisanal Ice Cream

Heartland's ice cream production emphasizes rich flavor and texture, starting with a high-quality base made fresh weekly. This base boasts a butterfat content typically around 16%, derived from the milk of the farm's own Holstein and Jersey herd, contributing to its exceptionally creamy consistency.

Production adheres to true small-batch principles, utilizing a 10-gallon batch freezer. This limited capacity means that typically only four to six different flavors are available at any given time, offered either in the farm store's dipping cabinet or pre-packaged in pint containers.

Popular flavors highlight local and high-quality ingredients:
*   **Pasture Perfect Vanilla Bean:** A classic favorite known for its rich taste.
*   **Chocolate Holstein Fudge:** Features a deep chocolate flavor achieved through a blend of fair-trade cocoa powder and 65% cacao dark chocolate sourced from Ethereal Confections, a craft chocolate maker in Illinois.
*   **Honey Lavender:** A sought-after summer seasonal flavor, infused with culinary lavender grown on-site at the farm and sweetened with local honey from Green Valley Apiaries, located less than 10 miles away.

Due to the small production runs and high demand, especially during the peak visitor season from June to August, popular pint flavors like 'Pasture Perfect Vanilla Bean' often sell out rapidly, sometimes within just two to three days of being stocked in the farm store freezer.

### Dedicated Production

While Head Cheesemaker Maria Sanchez oversees all dairy production at Heartland Family Farms Creamery, the day-to-day operations for butter and ice cream are managed by a dedicated team member specifically trained in these artisanal techniques, ensuring consistent quality and attention to detail for these non-cheese dairy delights.
