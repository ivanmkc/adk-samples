# Griffin Drone Series Specifications

The Griffin series represents Aetheria Dynamics' core line of autonomous aerial vehicles, designed for various logistics applications. The series includes models optimized for different payload capacities and operational requirements, all managed by the Helios AI platform.

## Griffin-SD (Standard Delivery)

The Griffin-SD is the standard model tailored for last-mile delivery tasks.
*   **Payload:** Designed to carry payloads up to 5 kilograms, suitable for small parcels, food items, and medical samples.
*   **Power System:** Utilizes a modular 48-volt lithium-ion battery pack system.
*   **Flight Endurance:** Capable of approximately 45 minutes of flight time under standard payload and weather conditions.
*   **Charging:** Aetheria's rapid charging infrastructure enables an 80% charge capacity in under 30 minutes.
*   **Speed:** Achieves a cruising speed of 80 km/h and can reach a maximum burst speed of 110 km/h.
*   **Noise Profile:** Operational noise during cruise flight is rated at approximately 65 dBA at a 15-meter distance.
*   **Landing Accuracy:** Features an autonomous landing system achieving accuracy within 10 centimeters on designated landing zones.

## Griffin-XL (Heavy-Lift)

The Griffin-XL is engineered for significantly larger cargo transport.
*   **Payload Capacity:** Boasts a maximum payload capacity of 500 kilograms.
*   **Operational Range:** Capable of operating over a range of 150 kilometers.
*   **Propulsion:** Features an eight-rotor configuration, with each rotor measuring approximately 1.8 meters in diameter.
*   **Noise Profile:** Generates operational noise levels closer to 75 dBA during cruise flight at a 15-meter distance.

## Common Series Specifications

All drones within the Griffin series share several core technological features and operational parameters:
*   **Sensor Suite:** Equipped with a standard sensor package including redundant differential GPS, inertial measurement units (IMUs), barometric altimeters, ADS-B transceivers for airspace awareness, and forward/downward-facing LiDAR systems for precision navigation and obstacle avoidance.
*   **Operating Altitude:** Under typical FAA Beyond Visual Line of Sight (BVLOS) waiver conditions, such as those in Austin, Griffin drones cruise at altitudes between 100 meters (approx. 330 feet) and 120 meters (approx. 400 feet) Above Ground Level (AGL).
*   **Weather Tolerance:** The airframes are rated for safe operation in sustained wind speeds up to 40 km/h (approx. 25 mph) and can operate in light precipitation conditions.
*   **Communication:** Utilize encrypted C-band and 4G/5G LTE communication links for command, control, and telemetry data, managed by the Helios AI. A satellite communication backup system provides redundancy.
*   **Maintenance:** Mandated routine maintenance inspections occur every 100 flight hours, encompassing checks of rotors, batteries, sensors, and overall structural integrity.
