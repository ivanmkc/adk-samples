# ChronoLeap Product Portfolio: Games and Licensing

ChronoLeap Gaming pursues a dual strategy, developing highly immersive historical simulation games for the consumer market while also licensing its core technology, the Continuum Engine, for educational and cultural applications. This portfolio leverages the company's expertise in historical accuracy, advanced simulation technology, and immersive experiences.

## Flagship Game Titles

ChronoLeap has released three critically acclaimed flagship titles, each exploring a distinct historical period with meticulous detail:

*   **Rome Resurgent:** ChronoLeap's first major release, 'Rome Resurgent,' achieved significant commercial success, selling over 3 million copies worldwide between 2027 and 2029. Set in ancient Rome, the game allows players to experience events like simulated chariot races within a detailed reconstruction of the Circus Maximus. These races feature advanced physics powered by the Continuum Engine v4.7.
*   **Edo Echoes:** This title transports players to late Tokugawa Shogunate Japan, specifically focusing on the years 1850-1868. 'Edo Echoes' emphasizes complex social stealth mechanics rooted in the period's strict etiquette and features a dynamic simulation of the renowned Yoshiwara pleasure district.
*   **Wild West Reclaimed:** Players explore a procedurally generated representation of the Dakota Territory circa 1876. The game allows interaction with AI-driven historical figures, such as Calamity Jane, and participation in activities like simulated gold prospecting. An expansion pack, 'Ghosts of the Frontier,' released in Q1 2028, introduced gameplay centered on investigating alleged supernatural events drawn from historical frontier newspapers.

A common feature across all three titles is the 'Historian Mode.' Developed in collaboration with ChronoLeap co-founder Dr. Eleanor Vance, this mode provides players with contextual pop-up information and direct access to relevant primary source materials from the extensive Chronos Database during gameplay, enhancing the educational value alongside the entertainment.

## Continuum Engine Licensing

Beyond game development, ChronoLeap licenses its proprietary Continuum Engine technology to external organizations, primarily in the educational and museum sectors.

*   **Museum Installations:** One prominent licensee is the British Museum in London. They utilize a customized Continuum Engine installation for their 'Walk Like an Egyptian' virtual exhibit, enabling visitors to explore a detailed reconstruction of the Temple of Karnak. ChronoLeap provides specialized development tools for museum licensees, allowing curators to integrate scans of their own artifacts directly into the simulation environment.
*   **Educational Use:** Stanford University's History Department licenses the Continuum Engine for postgraduate research. This allows students to run complex social and economic simulations based on historical data, particularly utilizing the Chronos Database's medieval European datasets.

Standard licensing agreements reflect the different use cases. Educational institutions typically face a per-seat annual fee, starting around $5,000 per installation. For museum exhibits, the model usually involves a project-based fee combined with a revenue-sharing agreement.

ChronoLeap's product portfolio demonstrates a commitment to leveraging its advanced historical simulation technology for both engaging entertainment experiences and valuable educational and cultural applications.
