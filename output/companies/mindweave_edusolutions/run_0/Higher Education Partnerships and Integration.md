# MindWeave's Higher Education Partnerships and Integration

MindWeave EduSolutions is actively engaging with the higher education sector through its Synapse Platform. As of early 2024, the platform has been adopted or is in pilot stages at over 15 higher education institutions across the United States, impacting an estimated 20,000 undergraduate students, primarily within large introductory courses.

## The University of California Psychology Pilot

A significant initiative demonstrating MindWeave's approach is the University of California pilot program for Psychology 101. Initiated in 2022, this program specifically involved the Berkeley, Los Angeles (UCLA), and San Diego (UCSD) campuses. The primary focus was on measuring student engagement metrics and tracking performance improvements compared to control groups using traditional methods. Key success metrics tracked during this pilot included student time-on-task within the Synapse platform, correlation analysis between platform engagement patterns and final course grades, and identifying statistically significant improvements in pre/post-assessment scores on core psychological concepts compared to sections using traditional textbooks and assignments.

## Seamless Technical Integration with University Systems

Deep integration with existing university infrastructure is crucial for successful adoption. MindWeave leverages its LTI 1.3 Advantage certification to ensure compatibility with major Learning Management Systems (LMS) such as Canvas, Blackboard Learn, and Moodle. This certification enables key features like:

*   **Automatic Grade Synchronization:** Seamless transfer of grades from Synapse activities to the university's official gradebook.
*   **Single Sign-On (SSO):** Convenient access for both students and faculty using their existing university credentials.
*   **Embedded Content:** Faculty using Canvas or Blackboard can directly embed specific Synapse Texts chapters or targeted QuestLearn skill modules within their online course structure.
*   **Real-time Data Access:** Instructors can track student completion and performance data in real-time and access Synapse's predictive intervention flags directly within their familiar LMS environment.

The typical integration process involves a dedicated MindWeave implementation team collaborating closely with the institution's IT department. This collaboration focuses on LTI configuration and Student Information System (SIS) integration for automated student rostering. Discipline-specific faculty workshops are also conducted, focusing on utilizing the Synapse educator dashboard and interpreting the student predictive analytics provided by the platform.

## Specialized Content for Higher Education Needs

Beyond general introductory courses, MindWeave is developing specialized modules tailored for higher education, particularly in STEM fields. Current developments include:

*   **Synapse Calculus Readiness:** Designed for pre-calculus assessment and providing adaptive remediation pathways.
*   **Athena CS Fundamentals:** Focuses on introductory Python programming concepts, integrated with automated code assessment and debugging support via the Athena Tutor.

Within these STEM modules, the Athena Tutors play a specific role. They are programmed to provide context-aware support for complex problem sets, particularly in foundational engineering mathematics. This support includes step-by-step guidance derived directly from the solution methods taught in the associated Synapse Text or linked university course materials.

## Expanding Reach and Future Directions

MindWeave continues to expand its footprint in higher education. Beyond the University of California system, the company is actively pursuing pilot programs with large community college districts in Texas and Florida. The goal is to adapt the Synapse Platform for high-enrollment developmental mathematics courses, with these pilots targeted to begin in the 2025-2026 academic year.

## Tiered Partnership Models

To accommodate varying institutional needs and resources, MindWeave offers tiered partnership packages. These range from standard LTI integration support to premium tiers. Premium options can include dedicated data analysts to assist with institutional research support and ongoing pedagogical consulting services for faculty aiming to maximize the impact of the Synapse Platform on student learning outcomes.
