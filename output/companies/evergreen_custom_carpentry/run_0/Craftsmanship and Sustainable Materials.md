# Craftsmanship and Sustainable Materials at Evergreen Custom Carpentry

Evergreen Custom Carpentry stands on the twin pillars of exceptional craftsmanship and a deep commitment to environmental responsibility. This dedication is evident in every stage of their process, from material selection and preparation to joinery techniques and finishing touches.

## Heirloom Quality Through Traditional Techniques

The longevity and beauty of Evergreen's work are rooted in time-honored woodworking practices. Traditional joinery methods are frequently employed to ensure structural integrity and lasting quality. Hand-cut dovetails, known for their strength and aesthetic appeal, are often used for drawers. For larger structural elements, robust mortise and tenon joints provide enduring stability, ensuring pieces are built to last for generations.

Precision is paramount in the workshop. Standards often demand tolerances within 1/64th of an inch, a testament to the skill involved. This accuracy is achieved through a blend of carefully calibrated modern machinery and the irreplaceable finesse of skilled handwork. Attention to detail extends to the visual harmony of the wood itself; grain matching across adjacent components like cabinet doors or drawer fronts is a hallmark of their work, creating a seamless and sophisticated look.

## Commitment to Sustainable Sourcing

Evergreen Custom Carpentry places a strong emphasis on using materials that are both beautiful and responsibly sourced. The company primarily utilizes hardwoods certified by the Forest Stewardship Council (FSC), guaranteeing that the wood comes from forests managed in an environmentally sound, socially beneficial, and economically viable manner.

Preference is given to North American species, particularly Black Walnut, White Oak, Hard Maple, and Cherry, sourced predominantly from managed forests within the Appalachian region. To ensure the integrity of their supply chain, Evergreen maintains direct relationships with specific lumber mills in Vermont and Pennsylvania. These mills are known for their transparent chain-of-custody documentation, verifying the sustainable origins of the wood. Founder Silas Blackwood often takes a personal role in selecting lumber for significant projects, meticulously examining grain patterns and color consistency to ensure the final piece meets the highest aesthetic standards.

## Meticulous Finishing and Waste Reduction

The finishing process is as carefully considered as the construction. A multi-stage hand-finishing process is standard practice. This typically involves meticulous sanding, often progressing up to a 400-grit finish, to achieve a perfectly smooth surface. The choice of finish prioritizes both durability and environmental consideration, favoring low-VOC (Volatile Organic Compound) conversion varnishes or natural oil finishes like Tung oil.

Sustainability extends to workshop protocols aimed at minimizing wood waste. Usable offcuts are thoughtfully repurposed, finding new life as components in smaller projects, workshop jigs, or are sometimes donated to local artisan programs, ensuring that the valuable resource is utilized as fully as possible.

## Quality Components and Skilled Artisans

Complementing the woodwork, the selection of hardware, such as hinges and pulls, focuses on high-quality materials like solid brass or stainless steel sourced from reputable manufacturers. These components are chosen not only for their durability and long-term function but also to aesthetically enhance the craftsmanship of the piece.

The perpetuation of these high standards is ensured through a rigorous apprenticeship program. Trainees undergo a comprehensive 3 to 4-year period, mastering advanced joinery, wood science, finishing techniques, and the proficient use of both traditional hand tools and modern woodworking machinery, cultivating the next generation of skilled artisans dedicated to quality and sustainability.
