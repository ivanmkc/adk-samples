# Client Involvement in Material and Finish Selection at Evergreen Custom Carpentry

A cornerstone of Evergreen Custom Carpentry's philosophy is a deeply collaborative design process, ensuring the final piece perfectly reflects the client's vision. A critical phase in this partnership is the client's direct involvement in selecting the specific materials and finishes for their custom project. This typically occurs after the final CAD drawings have been approved but strategically before the lumber order for the project is placed, allowing selections to inform the exact materials procured.

## The Dedicated Workshop Visit

Clients are invited to the Burlington, Vermont workshop for a dedicated material and finish selection meeting. These focused sessions usually last between 1.5 and 3 hours and are personally guided by either founder Silas Blackwood or the lead designer assigned to the project. This hands-on experience allows clients to connect directly with the materials that will form their unique piece.

## Selecting the Perfect Lumber

For significant components, such as dining tabletops or prominent cabinet faces, clients participate actively in the lumber selection. They might review 3 to 5 different slabs of the chosen wood species, like Appalachian Black Walnut. Guided by Evergreen's expertise, clients evaluate specific grain flow, unique figures (such as curl or quilting), and the placement of natural characteristics within the wood.

A key focus during this review is discussing how the grain pattern will be utilized across adjacent parts. For instance, ensuring a continuous, flowing grain across a set of cabinet doors or drawer fronts is paramount. Evergreen designers often use templates laid directly onto the chosen slab to demonstrate how the wood's features will translate into the finished piece. Notes and photos from this process, detailing desired grain orientation or feature placement, are often annotated directly onto the project's CAD drawings, providing clear guidance for the craftspeople during fabrication.

## Approving Finishes

Finish approval relies on tangible comparisons. Clients review physical sample boards, typically sized 12x12 inches. These boards showcase the selected wood species treated with 2-3 variations of the proposed stain or clear coat. This allows for a direct comparison of color depth and sheen levels, such as matte, satin, or semi-gloss, ensuring the final look meets expectations.

## Choosing Hardware

Hardware selection is also integrated into the workshop visit. Clients can examine physical samples of high-quality hardware options. This often includes traditional styles from brands like Horton Brasses, as well as functional hardware like soft-close hinges and drawer slides from manufacturers such as Blum and Salice. Reviewing manufacturer catalogues alongside physical samples helps clients make informed decisions.

## Addressing Complexity and Remote Needs

For particularly complex integrations, like a unique door style paired with specific hardware or a novel finish combination, Evergreen may create a small-scale physical mock-up section. This allows the client to review and approve the specific detail during their workshop visit.

Evergreen understands that not all clients can visit the Burlington workshop. For these situations, a remote selection process is facilitated using high-resolution photographs and video calls to showcase specific lumber slabs and finish samples. To ensure tactile approval, physical finish samples are mailed directly to the client.

## Formalizing the Choices

Following the selection meeting (whether in-person or remote), the client's final choices are meticulously documented. Specific wood slabs (identified by unique markings), precise finish formulas, and hardware models are formally recorded in an addendum to the project contract or on a dedicated selection sheet. The client provides a signature to approve these selections, ensuring complete clarity and agreement before fabrication commences.

This detailed involvement empowers clients, fosters a deeper connection to their project, and ensures that the final custom piece from Evergreen Custom Carpentry is not only expertly crafted but also a true reflection of personal taste and requirements.
