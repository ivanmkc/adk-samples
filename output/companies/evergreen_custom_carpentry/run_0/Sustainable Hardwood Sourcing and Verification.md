# Sustainable Hardwood Sourcing and Verification at Evergreen Custom Carpentry

Evergreen Custom Carpentry demonstrates a profound operational commitment to environmental responsibility through its rigorous approach to sustainable hardwood sourcing. Approximately 95% of the solid hardwood volume utilized annually by the company meets the stringent standards set forth by the Forest Stewardship Council (FSC). The company actively prioritizes sourcing hardwoods that carry the 'FSC 100%' or 'FSC Mix' labels, signifying that the wood originates from forests managed according to FSC's globally recognized principles.

## Rigorous Verification Procedures

Ensuring the authenticity of sustainability claims involves a multi-layered verification process. Central to this is the meticulous review of chain-of-custody (CoC) documentation that accompanies every lumber shipment. This documentation includes unique FSC CoC codes, which are traceable back to the specific certified forest source, providing a clear link from forest to workshop.

Upon arrival at the Burlington workshop, the verification continues with physical inspection. Lumber shipments are checked not only for quality standards but also for the presence of corresponding FSC stamps or labels on bundles or individual boards. This physical evidence is carefully cross-referenced against the delivery manifests and the accompanying CoC documents to ensure consistency and legitimacy.

Furthermore, Evergreen maintains comprehensive records of all FSC-certified lumber purchases. These records, which include supplier invoices and CoC certificates, are meticulously organized by project. This system ensures full traceability, crucial for potential FSC audits and for providing clients with specific sourcing information for their projects. For premium species frequently used in signature pieces, such as Appalachian Black Walnut sourced from their primary Pennsylvania mill partner, Evergreen specifically requests and verifies lot-specific FSC documentation, adding another layer of assurance.

## Supplier Management and Responsible Sourcing

Evergreen's commitment extends to its relationships with suppliers. Key lumber suppliers, including specific family-owned mills in Vermont and Pennsylvania, undergo a thorough vetting process. This includes confirming their active FSC certification status and reviewing their CoC procedures on an annual basis.

The company focuses its sourcing efforts on the well-managed temperate forests of the Appalachian region, known for producing high-quality species like White Oak and Black Cherry. Suppliers are specifically chosen based on their demonstrated adherence to FSC Principles, particularly those concerning biodiversity protection and the maintenance of ecosystem health within the forests they manage. Beyond formal certification, Evergreen engages in discussions with its mill partners about sustainable harvesting techniques, such as the selective cutting practices employed in the Appalachian forests from which they source their lumber.

## Transparency with Clients

Evergreen Custom Carpentry proactively communicates its dedication to FSC-certified sourcing to its clients, typically during the material selection phase of a project. Understanding the importance of transparency, the company often provides clients with copies of the relevant CoC documentation pertaining to the specific lumber used in their project upon request, reinforcing trust and demonstrating their commitment to sustainable practices.
