# Evergreen Custom Carpentry: Bespoke Kitchen Cabinetry Solutions

Evergreen Custom Carpentry specializes in crafting high-end, fully custom kitchen cabinetry solutions tailored to the unique needs and aesthetic visions of clients throughout New England. Combining meticulous craftsmanship with advanced design capabilities, the company creates kitchens that are both exceptionally functional and enduringly beautiful.

## Collaborative Design and Material Excellence

The creation of an Evergreen kitchen begins with a deeply collaborative design process. Utilizing detailed CAD drawings and 3D renderings, the team works closely with clients and designers to translate concepts into precise plans. This process allows for the exploration of distinct design directions, such as a signature high-contrast style pairing painted Hard Maple perimeter cabinetry (often finished in a durable low-VOC conversion varnish matched to colors like Benjamin Moore 'White Dove') with a striking central island crafted from quarter-sawn White Oak, enhanced by a hand-rubbed Tung oil finish.

Material selection emphasizes sustainably sourced North American hardwoods like Hard Maple, White Oak, and Appalachian Cherry. Cabinet carcass construction typically employs robust dado joinery for fixed shelves and strong blind mortise and tenon joints for face frames. This ensures exceptional structural rigidity, capable of supporting heavy stone countertops which can weigh upwards of 15-20 pounds per square foot.

## Precision Integration and Specialized Features

A hallmark of Evergreen's kitchen cabinetry is the seamless integration of appliances and specialized features. Using manufacturer-provided CAD templates and precise field measurements, openings for integrated appliances like Sub-Zero refrigerators or Wolf wall ovens are meticulously crafted. Adjustable mounting points are often incorporated to achieve the consistent sub-1/32nd inch reveals required for flawless panel integration.

Custom kitchen islands frequently serve as centerpieces, incorporating features like integrated end-grain Hard Maple butcher blocks. These blocks, typically 2.5 to 3 inches thick, are seamlessly inset into the primary countertop material (such as Quartz or Granite) and finished with a food-safe mineral oil and beeswax blend. Functionality is further enhanced with built-in charging stations, often featuring recessed power hubs with USB-A and USB-C ports from brands like Docking Drawer, discreetly located within drawers or beneath countertop overhangs for clutter-free utility.

Specialized storage solutions are designed to maximize space and accessibility. Examples include full-extension pantry pull-outs utilizing heavy-duty slides rated for over 100 pounds (like Blum Tandembox systems), sophisticated corner cabinet solutions such as Lemans II swing-out units, and custom-fitted drawer organizers crafted from matching wood species.

## Meticulous Finishing and Hardware Detailing

The multi-step finishing process is critical for both aesthetics and durability. It includes the meticulous sealing of all interior cabinet surfaces, drawer boxes, and shelf edges with the same durable low-VOC conversion varnish used externally. This comprehensive approach maximizes moisture resistance, essential in a kitchen environment, and meets rigorous KCMA (Kitchen Cabinet Manufacturers Association) standards for durability and resistance.

Cabinet designs thoughtfully account for details like under-cabinet lighting. Solutions often involve recessed channels milled directly into the underside of wall cabinets or integrated light valances specifically designed to conceal low-profile LED tape lighting systems from suppliers like Hafele Loox. For traditional styles, such as inset cabinet doors popular in New England kitchens, high-quality solid brass butt hinges from manufacturers like Horton Brasses are commonly specified, meticulously mortised flush into both the door and face frame for precise alignment and smooth operation.

## Seamless Project Coordination

Evergreen understands that successful kitchen projects require coordination. The company collaborates closely with stone fabricators chosen by the client, providing precise final CAD files (typically in .dwg format) of the installed cabinet layout. This ensures accurate digital templating for countertops, which is especially crucial for complex island shapes or integrated sink cutouts, contributing to a flawless final installation.

Through a combination of bespoke design, premium materials, robust construction, thoughtful features, and meticulous execution, Evergreen Custom Carpentry delivers kitchen cabinetry solutions that embody lasting quality and personalized style.
