# Architectural Millwork and Restoration at Evergreen Custom Carpentry

Evergreen Custom Carpentry possesses deep expertise in the specialized field of architectural millwork, encompassing both the meticulous restoration of historic woodwork and the creation of bespoke millwork packages for contemporary high-end residences throughout New England. Operating from their Burlington, Vermont workshop, the company blends traditional craftsmanship with modern precision to produce elements that define architectural character.

## Historical Restoration and Reproduction

A significant aspect of Evergreen's work involves the faithful reproduction of complex historical millwork profiles. The company frequently undertakes projects in historically sensitive areas, such as Boston's Beacon Hill and Newport, Rhode Island, replicating intricate details like Georgian crown moldings or Federal style window casings. This demanding work relies on meticulously matching original surviving samples or detailed architectural drawings.

Collaboration with preservation architects is key, often involving detailed site surveys and analysis of existing woodwork. This informs the creation of precise shop drawings for reproduction millwork, ensuring compliance with strict historic district guidelines. To achieve authenticity, Evergreen utilizes custom-ground knives for their industrial moulder, allowing for the precise replication of unique or historical molding profiles. This commitment extends to structural elements as well; the company can source and mill large-dimension timbers, such as White Oak or Douglas Fir, adhering to historical specifications for beams or posts, often employing traditional mortise and tenon joinery for structural integrity.

## Custom Millwork for Luxury Residences

Beyond restoration, Evergreen fabricates comprehensive custom millwork packages for new construction and renovations, particularly within the luxury residential market. These projects range significantly in scale, from crafting a single, ornate fireplace mantelpiece—sometimes incorporating character-rich reclaimed timbers from historic New England barns—to providing complete interior trim packages for multi-million dollar homes across the region.

Such packages often include hundreds of linear feet of bespoke baseboards, casings, and complex multi-piece crown moldings. Evergreen also produces custom wainscoting systems in various styles, including raised panel, flat panel, and beadboard. Sections are often pre-assembled in the Burlington workshop to ensure consistent reveals, precise alignment, and efficient installation on-site. Another area of expertise is the fabrication of custom solid wood coffered ceiling systems. Evergreen engineers the grid layout and crafts the beam components and inset panels from premium species like White Oak or Cherry, often integrating lighting solutions for grand residential spaces.

## Materials, Techniques, and Finishing

The choice of materials is tailored to the project's requirements. Paint-grade architectural millwork is typically crafted from stable hardwoods like Poplar or Hard Maple. Stain-grade projects often feature premium Appalachian Cherry or White Oak. Regardless of the material, the millwork receives the same meticulous multi-stage finishing process applied to Evergreen's renowned cabinetry. This involves careful preparation and the application of durable, low-VOC conversion varnishes, sprayed in custom colors within the dedicated Burlington finishing booth to achieve a flawless surface. The precision required for both restoration and new custom work is achieved through a combination of skilled handwork and advanced machinery, including the use of custom-ground knives on industrial moulders to ensure sharp details and accurate profiles.

## Future Enhancements

Looking ahead, Evergreen Custom Carpentry plans to further enhance its capabilities in complex millwork production. The planned acquisition of a 5-axis CNC router by 2026 is expected to significantly improve efficiency and precision in producing intricate curved moldings, carved architectural details like capitals or brackets, and the radius work often required for high-end custom designs.

In summary, Evergreen Custom Carpentry offers comprehensive architectural millwork services, distinguished by a commitment to historical accuracy in restoration, exceptional craftsmanship in new custom work, and the use of premium materials and finishes. Their ability to handle projects ranging from single elements to complete residential packages makes them a valued partner for discerning clients, architects, and designers throughout New England.
