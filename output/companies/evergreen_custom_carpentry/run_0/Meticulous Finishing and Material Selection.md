# Meticulous Finishing and Material Selection at Evergreen Custom Carpentry

Evergreen Custom Carpentry's reputation for exceptional quality is built upon a foundation of uncompromising standards in material selection and finishing. Every step, from the arrival of raw lumber to the final coat of finish, is executed with precision and care, ensuring both aesthetic beauty and long-lasting durability.

## Premium Material Sourcing and Preparation

The process begins with the careful selection of raw materials. Evergreen typically specifies FAS (First and Seconds) grade lumber, the highest standard grade established by the National Hardwood Lumber Association (NHLA), for primary visual surfaces. This ensures minimal natural defects and allows the optimal grain quality of the wood to shine through. Sourcing focuses on Forest Stewardship Council (FSC) certified hardwoods, primarily North American species from managed forests.

Upon arrival at the Burlington workshop, all incoming FSC-certified lumber undergoes a crucial acclimatization period. Boards, especially wider stock destined for tabletops, are carefully stickered and stacked within the workshop's climate-controlled environment. This process lasts a minimum of 4-6 weeks, allowing the wood's moisture content to stabilize relative to the workshop conditions, minimizing potential movement after milling and assembly.

For projects demanding visual continuity, such as large library walls or conference tables, Evergreen meticulously sequences boards cut from the same log. These boards are often labeled during the milling process to maintain a continuous, flowing grain pattern across the entire assembled piece, showcasing the natural beauty of the wood.

## Advanced Finishing Techniques

The finishing process at Evergreen is a multi-stage endeavor designed to achieve a flawless and durable surface. Initial sanding progresses up to a fine 400-grit smoothness. Following this, a grain-filling sealer is often applied to create a perfectly smooth, non-porous base, essential for the subsequent topcoats.

To minimize wood movement and ensure finish consistency, vulnerable areas like the end grain on solid wood countertops or door panels are meticulously sealed. This is often done with a diluted application of the primary finish before the main finishing coats are applied.

Evergreen utilizes a dedicated finishing room engineered to optimize application and curing. This space features positive pressure ventilation with HEPA filtration to eliminate dust contamination and maintains stable humidity (around 40-50% RH) and temperature (65-70°F) year-round.

Clients can choose from several premium finish options. The standard choice is a low-VOC (Volatile Organic Compound) conversion varnish, selected to meet or exceed the rigorous Kitchen Cabinet Manufacturers Association (KCMA) standards for chemical resistance, water resistance, and overall durability. This ensures longevity, particularly in demanding environments like kitchens. For projects requiring paint, custom colors are precisely matched using spectrophotometer readings and formulas compatible with major paint systems (e.g., Benjamin Moore, Sherwin-Williams). The paint is then expertly sprayed within the dedicated finishing booth for a flawless, durable surface.

Alternatively, natural Tung oil finishes are favored for pieces where a hand-rubbed, close-to-the-wood feel is desired, such as walnut dining tables. This traditional process typically involves applying 3-5 coats by hand over several days, allowing each coat to partially cure before the next is applied. Clients receive detailed instructions for the long-term care and re-application of these natural oil finishes.

## High-Quality Hardware Integration

Complementing the woodwork, hardware selection receives equal attention. Evergreen frequently specifies high-end European brands like Blum or Grass for concealed hinges and drawer slides, renowned for their precision engineering and smooth, soft-close mechanisms. For pieces requiring period-appropriate aesthetics, decorative hardware from respected US makers like Horton Brasses is often selected, ensuring functional integrity aligns with the design style.

Through this rigorous attention to material quality, preparation, environmental control, finishing techniques, and hardware selection, Evergreen Custom Carpentry ensures each piece not only meets but exceeds client expectations for beauty, function, and enduring quality.
