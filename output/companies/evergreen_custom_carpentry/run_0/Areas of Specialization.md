# Evergreen Custom Carpentry: Areas of Specialization

Evergreen Custom Carpentry excels in a diverse range of high-end woodworking applications, demonstrating mastery across several key areas. From intricate kitchen cabinetry to unique furniture pieces and detailed architectural elements, the company leverages deep expertise and premium materials.

## Custom Kitchen Cabinetry

A significant area of focus is custom kitchen cabinetry. Evergreen frequently designs and builds cabinetry systems that seamlessly integrate high-end appliances from renowned brands such as Sub-Zero, Wolf, and Miele. This work demands exceptional precision, often requiring fitting tolerances tighter than 1/32nd of an inch to achieve a flawless result. Custom kitchen islands are another hallmark, often incorporating specialized features tailored to client needs, such as integrated end-grain Hard Maple butcher blocks, custom-configured spice racks, or discreet built-in charging stations. For painted kitchen projects, the company typically utilizes stable paint-grade hardwoods like Poplar or Hard Maple, finished with durable, low-VOC conversion varnishes meticulously matched to custom colors specified by the client or their designer.

## Built-in Furniture

Evergreen is adept at creating bespoke built-in furniture that enhances both the form and function of a space. Popular projects include stunning floor-to-ceiling library walls, commonly crafted from select North American hardwoods like White Oak or Cherry. These installations sometimes feature elegant rolling ladders sourced from specialized suppliers to complete the classic aesthetic. Window seat constructions are also frequently commissioned, designed with integrated storage solutions that maximize utility. These often incorporate high-quality soft-close drawer slides or specialized lid-stay hinges for convenient lift-up seat access.

## Architectural Millwork

The company possesses considerable expertise in crafting complex architectural millwork. This includes producing historically accurate reproductions of crown moldings, wainscoting, and window casings, essential for restoration projects within New England's rich historic districts. Millwork projects vary significantly in scale, ranging from the creation of a single custom fireplace mantelpiece, potentially incorporating characterful reclaimed lumber, to outfitting entire luxury residences with bespoke baseboards, casings, and elaborate coffered ceilings.

## One-of-a-Kind Furniture

Evergreen undertakes commissions for unique, standalone furniture pieces. Notable examples include large, single-slab live-edge dining tables, often crafted from sustainably sourced Vermont Black Walnut or Pennsylvania Cherry, with some pieces exceeding 10 to 12 feet in length. These one-of-a-kind creations, sometimes personally designed by founder Silas Blackwood, serve as focal points and often showcase advanced joinery techniques like intricate hand-cut dovetails or decorative marquetry. Highly figured woods such as Bird's Eye Maple or Quilted Cherry are frequently selected to add exceptional visual appeal to these signature pieces.

## Custom Doors

The workshop also produces custom solid wood interior and exterior doors. Employing robust traditional joinery methods like mortise and tenon joints ensures structural integrity and longevity. Evergreen offers a range of styles, from traditional raised panel doors suitable for historic homes to sleek, modern flush designs, catering to diverse architectural aesthetics.
