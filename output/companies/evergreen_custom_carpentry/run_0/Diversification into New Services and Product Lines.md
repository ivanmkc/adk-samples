# Evergreen Custom Carpentry: Diversifying into New Services and Product Lines

Building upon its three-decade legacy of exceptional custom woodworking and a stellar reputation throughout New England, Evergreen Custom Carpentry is strategically expanding its offerings. Under the guidance of founder Silas Blackwood, the company is diversifying into new product lines and specialized services, leveraging its master craftsmanship, commitment to sustainable materials, and the expanded capacity of its Burlington, Vermont workshop.

## The Evergreen Signature Furniture Line

A significant new venture is the development of the Evergreen Signature Furniture line, designed by Silas Blackwood himself. This limited-edition collection aims to capture the essence of Evergreen's craftsmanship in standalone pieces.

*   **Launch and Production:** The initial launch is targeted for Q4 2025, coinciding with the completion of the Burlington workshop expansion. Production will occur in dedicated batch runs, typically 10-15 units per design, assigned to a select team of senior craftspeople. This approach ensures consistent quality without disrupting ongoing custom projects.
*   **Exclusivity and Authenticity:** Each piece will be individually numbered and accompanied by a certificate of authenticity signed by Silas Blackwood. This certificate will detail the specific FSC-certified Appalachian hardwoods used, reinforcing the company's commitment to sustainability and traceability.
*   **Materials and Pricing:** Reflecting the premium materials, design exclusivity, and master craftsmanship, pricing for initial pieces, such as the Appalachian Black Walnut dining table, is projected to range from $18,000 to $28,000.
*   **Marketing:** Initial marketing efforts will focus on exclusive preview events held at the Burlington workshop. These events will target Evergreen's established high-end clientele and partner architectural and design firms across New England.

## Standalone Design Consultation Service

Leveraging Silas Blackwood's extensive expertise, Evergreen plans to launch a formal Design Consultation service in early 2026.

*   **Offering:** Personally led by Silas Blackwood, this service will provide expert advice on material selection, joinery, and structural integrity for wood-centric projects.
*   **Target Audience and Pricing:** The service targets architects, designers, and homeowners seeking specialized woodworking knowledge, with a proposed hourly rate of $300.
*   **Deliverables:** Clients can expect detailed written reports with recommendations, annotated concept sketches, and curated physical samples of suggested premium wood species and finishes drawn from Evergreen's extensive stock.
*   **Promotion:** Evergreen intends to promote this service through targeted outreach presentations and informational materials distributed to members of the New England chapters of the American Institute of Architects (AIA) and the American Society of Interior Designers (ASID).

## Specialized Wood Finishing and Restoration Service

Complementing its custom build services, Evergreen is introducing a specialized Wood Finishing and Restoration service.

*   **Focus:** This service will concentrate on antique American furniture (primarily pre-1950s) and architectural woodwork within historic New England properties.
*   **Techniques and Resources:** Utilizing traditional techniques, the service will employ specialized skills such as multi-step French polishing for fine antiques and meticulous color-matching using period-appropriate pigments for historic elements like fireplace mantels or built-in cabinetry. A dedicated bench area and specialized tooling within the newly expanded finishing section of the Burlington workshop will support these intricate tasks.
*   **Assessment:** An initial assessment fee of $150, applicable towards the final project cost, will cover the detailed evaluation and documentation required for restoration projects, typically conducted at the workshop.

These strategic diversifications represent a natural evolution for Evergreen Custom Carpentry, broadening its reach while remaining firmly rooted in the principles of quality, sustainability, and master craftsmanship that have defined the company for decades.
