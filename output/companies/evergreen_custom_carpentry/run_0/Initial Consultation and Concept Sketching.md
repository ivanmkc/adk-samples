## The Initial Consultation and Concept Sketching Phase

The journey towards a custom piece from Evergreen Custom Carpentry begins with a crucial initial consultation, designed to establish a clear understanding between the client and the design team. This foundational step sets the stage for the entire project.

### Setting the Stage: The On-Site Meeting

Typically lasting between one and two hours, the initial consultation takes place directly at the client's property. This on-site approach is essential for projects within Evergreen's New England service area. It allows the Evergreen representative, often founder Silas Blackwood himself, to experience the space firsthand.

### Gathering Information and Vision

During this meeting, several key activities occur. Detailed measurements of the site are meticulously taken. Equally important is the in-depth discussion covering the client's specific functional requirements for the piece, their aesthetic preferences and overall vision, desired materials (such as specific Appalachian hardwoods), and the preliminary budget for the project.

### The Power of Hand-Drawn Sketches

A distinctive feature of Evergreen's initial consultation is the frequent creation of hand-drawn concept sketches *during* the meeting itself. Using tools like graphite pencils and trace paper, the designer can rapidly visualize ideas and engage in collaborative brainstorming with the client.

The primary advantage of this initial hand-sketching process is its fluidity. It allows for the quick exploration of multiple design possibilities – perhaps different layouts for kitchen cabinets or varying configurations for built-in bookshelves – before committing time and resources to more formal digital designs.

### Visualizing the Concept

These initial sketches are more than just rough ideas. They often include basic floor plans, elevation views, and simple perspective drawings. This helps communicate the intended scale, form, and placement of the proposed custom piece within the context of the client's existing space, facilitating a shared understanding.

### Informed by Context: The Site Assessment

The site visit component is critical. Assessing the architectural context, existing lighting conditions, surrounding furniture and finishes, and any potential installation challenges directly informs the sketches being produced. This ensures the initial concepts are grounded in the reality of the space.

### Collaborative Refinement

Client feedback is actively sought and gathered throughout the sketching process during this initial meeting. This interactive approach enables the Evergreen designer to make real-time modifications to the drawings, responding directly to the client's reactions and suggestions, fostering a truly collaborative design start.

### Key Participants

Attendees at this important first meeting typically include the primary homeowners. Frequently, if an architect or interior designer is involved in the broader project, they will also attend to ensure alignment among all key decision-makers from the very beginning.

### From Sketch to Formal Design

Following the consultation, the finalized hand-drawn sketches, along with detailed notes from the discussion, become the primary reference material. They form the basis for developing formal project proposals and, subsequently, the detailed CAD drawings that will be presented for further client review and approval.

### An Integrated Part of the Service

Evergreen Custom Carpentry generally includes this initial consultation and the preliminary concept sketching as an integral part of their comprehensive service for commissioned projects within New England. Rather than charging a separate upfront design fee, it is considered the essential first step in their collaborative process.
