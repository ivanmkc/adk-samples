# Evergreen Custom Carpentry: Future Vision and Growth

Building upon a three-decade legacy of master craftsmanship and exceptional quality, Evergreen Custom Carpentry is strategically planning for future growth and innovation. While maintaining its core commitment to bespoke woodworking and sustainable practices, the company envisions significant developments in its facilities, technology, service offerings, and market reach.

## Expanding Capabilities: Workshop and Technology

A cornerstone of Evergreen's growth strategy is the planned expansion of its Burlington workshop. This expansion aims to add approximately 2,500 square feet, significantly enhancing operational capacity. Key features will include a larger, climate-controlled finishing area, crucial for maintaining the high standards of its multi-stage finishing process, and dedicated space for assembling larger-scale architectural millwork projects.

Complementing the physical expansion is a commitment to technological advancement. By 2026, Evergreen plans to acquire a 5-axis CNC router. This sophisticated machinery will enhance precision for complex joinery and intricate carvings, augmenting the company's renowned traditional handcraftsmanship rather than replacing it.

## Diversifying Services and Offerings

Evergreen envisions broadening its service portfolio. Plans are underway to offer standalone woodworking design consultation services, spearheaded by founder Silas Blackwood. This service will target architects and homeowners needing expert guidance on material selection, joinery techniques, and structural considerations for wood-centric designs, potentially offered on an hourly basis. Furthermore, the company is exploring the addition of specialized wood finishing and restoration services for antique furniture and existing architectural woodwork, complementing its core custom build capabilities.

A significant future initiative is the launch of a signature furniture line. Designed by Silas Blackwood, this line will initially feature 3-5 core pieces, such as a dining table and sideboard. These pieces will be produced in limited, numbered batches (e.g., 10-15 units per design) using premium Appalachian hardwoods, embodying the company's dedication to exclusivity and quality.

## Deepening Sustainability and Material Innovation

Evergreen plans to further enhance its strong sustainability profile. A dedicated program is being developed to source and utilize high-quality reclaimed lumber from historic New England structures for specific commissions. Additionally, the company intends to research the structural and aesthetic applications of sustainable engineered wood products, such as cross-laminated timber (CLT) panels, exploring their potential integration into larger architectural projects.

## Investing in Talent and Partnerships

Recognizing the importance of skilled craftspeople, Evergreen aims to potentially double its apprenticeship intake within the next five years. To support this, the company plans to formalize partnerships with Vermont technical colleges, creating a robust pipeline for future talent trained in the Evergreen tradition of excellence.

Strategic partnerships are also key to future growth. Evergreen aims to establish formal collaborations with at least two leading New England architectural firms known for luxury residential design by 2027. These partnerships are envisioned to facilitate a steady stream of high-end project collaborations.

## Enhancing Market Presence

To attract clients beyond its traditional New England base, Evergreen plans to invest in high-definition videography and immersive 3D virtual tours. These digital assets will showcase the Burlington workshop and completed projects, significantly enhancing the company's online portfolio and demonstrating its capabilities to a wider audience.

Through these focused initiatives—spanning facility upgrades, technological integration, service diversification, talent development, and strategic partnerships—Evergreen Custom Carpentry is poised for continued success, blending its rich heritage of craftsmanship with a dynamic vision for the future.
