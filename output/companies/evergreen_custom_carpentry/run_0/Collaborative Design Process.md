# The Evergreen Collaborative Design Process

At Evergreen Custom Carpentry, the creation of bespoke woodworking pieces is fundamentally a partnership with the client. The design process is intentionally collaborative, ensuring the final product perfectly aligns with the client's vision, needs, and space. This journey involves several key stages, inviting client participation from conception to completion.

## Initial Consultation and Concept Development

The process typically commences with an in-depth initial consultation. Often held on-site at the client's location within the New England service area, this meeting is led by founder Silas Blackwood or a senior designer. This allows for a thorough understanding of the project scope, the client's aesthetic preferences, and the specific context of the space.

Following the consultation, initial design concepts frequently take shape as hand-drawn sketches. This traditional approach fosters a fluid discussion and allows for rapid iteration alongside the client. Ideas can be explored and refined quickly before committing significant time to digital modeling, ensuring the core design direction resonates with the client early on.

## Detailed Design and Visualization

For projects demanding intricate detail, such as custom kitchens or complex built-in units, Evergreen transitions the design into Computer-Aided Design (CAD) software. This step produces precise 2D drawings and immersive 3D renderings. These detailed visuals provide clients with a clear understanding of the proposed piece's scale, proportions, and integration into their environment, facilitating informed approval before construction begins.

## Material Selection and Finish Approval

A hallmark of the Evergreen process is the client's direct involvement in material selection. Clients are welcomed to the Burlington workshop to personally engage with the materials. This includes selecting specific wood species and even reviewing individual lumber slabs to appreciate grain patterns and color variations.

This hands-on approach extends to choosing hardware. Clients can review catalogues or handle physical samples of high-quality hinges, pulls, and knobs, often sourced from brands known for durability and aesthetic synergy with fine woodworking.

Crucially, clients also approve finish samples. Evergreen provides physical samples demonstrating the chosen wood species treated with the exact finishing process planned for their project – including the specific sanding level, stain color, and topcoat sheen (e.g., varnish). This ensures complete clarity and satisfaction with the final look and feel.

## Milestones, Communication, and Final Review

Formal review meetings are strategically scheduled at key project milestones. These occur after initial sketches are presented, upon completion of CAD drawings, and before lumber is ordered, among other points. These meetings serve as crucial checkpoints to ensure ongoing alignment, incorporate feedback, and secure client approval before proceeding to the next phase.

Throughout the build phase, Evergreen maintains regular communication with the client. Progress photos from the workshop are often shared via email, supplemented by phone calls as needed. This keeps clients informed and engaged between the more formal review stages.

Before the scheduled installation, a final site measure and walkthrough are frequently conducted. This step, involving the client or their representative, confirms site readiness and addresses any last-minute, site-specific considerations to ensure a smooth installation process.

The collaborative journey culminates in a final walkthrough after the piece has been installed. This allows the client to review the finished work in its intended space, ensuring complete satisfaction with the craftsmanship, the design execution, and its seamless integration into their home or business.
