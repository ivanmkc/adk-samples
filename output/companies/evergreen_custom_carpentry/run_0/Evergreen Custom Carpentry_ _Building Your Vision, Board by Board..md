# Evergreen Custom Carpentry: Building Your Vision, Board by Board.

For approximately three decades, Evergreen Custom Carpentry has stood as a beacon of fine woodworking in New England. Founded by master craftsman Silas Blackwood, the company operates from its dedicated workshop in Burlington, Vermont, embodying its mission: 'Building Your Vision, Board by Board.'

## Services and Expertise

Evergreen Custom Carpentry serves the entire New England region, catering to the needs of both residential and commercial clients. The company has carved a niche in high-end custom woodworking, specializing in several key areas:
*   **Custom Kitchen Cabinetry:** Designing and building bespoke cabinetry solutions tailored to individual kitchens.
*   **Built-in Furniture:** Crafting integrated pieces such as elegant bookshelves and cozy window seats that enhance architectural spaces.
*   **Architectural Millwork:** Producing detailed trim, molding, and other architectural wood elements.
*   **One-of-a-Kind Furniture:** Creating unique, standalone furniture pieces designed to client specifications.

## Philosophy and Process

At the heart of Evergreen Custom Carpentry lies a deep commitment to quality and sustainability. A core principle is the exclusive use of sustainably sourced hardwoods, ensuring environmental responsibility alongside exceptional material quality.

The creation process is highly collaborative. Clients are involved from the very beginning, starting with initial sketches and continuing through detailed design discussions, material selection, and finally, the meticulous installation of the finished piece. This ensures the final product truly reflects the client's vision.

Internally, the company culture fosters meticulous craftsmanship and precision. There is a strong emphasis on the value of apprenticeship, ensuring that traditional woodworking skills are passed down and honed, maintaining the high standards set by Silas Blackwood.

## Market Position and Reputation

Operating in a competitive market, Evergreen Custom Carpentry distinguishes itself from other custom woodworking shops, larger cabinet manufacturers, and high-end furniture retailers. It has successfully built a stellar reputation among its clientele and within the New England design community, recognized for its exceptional quality, attention to detail, and highly personalized service.

## Future Directions

Looking ahead, Evergreen Custom Carpentry has plans for growth. These include expanding the workshop space in Burlington to accommodate increasing demand and potentially developing a small, curated line of signature furniture pieces intended for limited production runs.

Evergreen Custom Carpentry continues its legacy, combining traditional techniques with sustainable practices and a collaborative spirit to transform wood into lasting works of functional art.
