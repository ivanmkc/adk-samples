# Prairie Hearth Explores Packaged Goods: Mixes and Frozen Lines

Building on two decades of success with artisanal breads and pastries, Prairie Hearth Bakery Collective is actively developing a new line of packaged goods designed to bring their signature flavors and commitment to quality ingredients into home kitchens. This expansion focuses initially on baking mixes based on popular recipes and explores the potential for frozen bake-at-home items.

## Baking Mix Development

The development team is crafting mixes that reflect the bakery's core offerings and sourcing principles.

*   **'North Star Sourdough' Mix:** A key feature of this mix will be the inclusion of a dehydrated sourdough starter culture derived directly from "Goldie," the bakery's renowned 20-year-old starter. Recognizing the unique nature of this ingredient, the team is developing detailed activation instructions for home users.
*   **'Wild Rice & Cranberry Loaf' Mix:** This mix aims to replicate the beloved bakery loaf by including pre-portioned packets of key regional ingredients: dried, unsweetened cranberries sourced from growers near Warrens, Wisconsin, and parched wild rice obtained from Northern Minnesota indigenous harvesters. This mirrors the careful sourcing established for the fresh version.
*   **Heirloom Grain Focus:** Both mixes will feature the high-quality heirloom grains Prairie Hearth is known for, such as organic Red Fife and Turkey Red wheat. Preliminary pricing discussions by the Finance Committee project a retail price point between $10 and $12 per unit, reflecting the premium cost associated with these specific ingredients.

**Sourcing and Packaging:**

Securing sufficient ingredients is crucial. The Farm Relations Committee is actively negotiating contracts with two specific farms identified in late 2024: an organic spelt grower near Rochester, MN, and a Red Fife grower in western Wisconsin. The goal is to secure an additional 10,000 pounds of heirloom grains annually to support the 2025 mix launch.

Sustainability extends to the packaging itself. Prairie Hearth's Packaging Subcommittee, established in late 2023, is tasked with sourcing compostable or recyclable packaging materials, prioritizing suppliers within the Midwest. The packaging designs will also highlight key farm partnerships, such as their relationship with Meadowlark Organics.

**Pilot Launch:**

An initial pilot launch for the baking mixes is planned for the second quarter of 2025. This launch will specifically target three Minneapolis food cooperatives known for their customer base alignment with Prairie Hearth's values: Seward Community Co-op, Wedge Community Co-op, and Linden Hills Co-op.

## Frozen Bake-at-Home Line Exploration

Alongside the mixes, Prairie Hearth is investigating the feasibility of a frozen product line.

*   **Scones:** Initial development centers on adapting the bakery's popular seasonal fruit scone recipes. Trials are underway to ensure the scones maintain proper texture after freezing and subsequent baking in standard home ovens. A minimum frozen shelf life of three months is targeted.
*   **Croissants:** A potential frozen croissant line presents unique technical challenges. The team is evaluating specialized rapid freezing techniques, such as blast freezing, to determine if the integrity of the delicate butter lamination – crucial for achieving acceptable flakiness after consumer baking – can be preserved.

## Team and Oversight

Worker-owner Miguel Hernandez, recognized for his baking expertise, leads the technical recipe adaptation team for both the baking mix and potential frozen product lines. He provides quarterly progress updates on this development work to the cooperative's General Assembly, ensuring worker-owner oversight consistent with Prairie Hearth's governance model. Various committees, including Packaging, Finance, and Farm Relations, play integral roles in managing specific aspects of this new venture.
