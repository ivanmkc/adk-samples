# Cultivating Quality: Prairie Hearth's Key Farm Partnerships

Prairie Hearth Bakery Collective's commitment to artisanal quality and strengthening the regional food system is deeply rooted in its direct relationships with key farm partners. These partnerships go beyond simple transactions, involving collaboration, shared values, and a mutual dedication to exceptional ingredients. Sourcing directly from specific farms allows the bakery to secure unique heirloom grains, high-quality dairy, and other essential ingredients that define their signature products.

## Meadowlark Organics: Wisconsin's Red Fife Source

Since approximately 2008, Prairie Hearth has partnered with Meadowlark Organics, located in Ridgeway, Wisconsin. This relationship centers on securing high-quality, certified organic Red Fife wheat, an heirloom variety prized for its baking characteristics. Prairie Hearth typically purchases between 5,000 and 7,000 pounds of Red Fife annually from Meadowlark. To ensure supply and provide stability for the farm, the bakery often engages in pre-season contracts, committing to purchase a significant minimum volume (e.g., 80% of projected need) at a pre-agreed price range.

## Hazzard Free Farm: Minnesota Grains and Milling Expertise

A cornerstone relationship, established shortly after Prairie Hearth's founding in 2004, is with Hazzard Free Farm in Pequot Lakes, Minnesota. This partnership guarantees the bakery a consistent supply of MOSA-certified organic rye and spelt. A unique aspect of this collaboration is that Hazzard Free Farm often mills these grains on-farm using their stone mill, specifically tailoring the process for Prairie Hearth's orders. The bakery specifically selects Turkey Red wheat from Hazzard Free Farm for its robust flavor and targeted protein content (typically 13-14%), which performs exceptionally well in their long sourdough fermentation processes.

## Cedar Summit Farm: Exclusive Grass-Fed Dairy

Prairie Hearth has sourced 100% of its dairy products exclusively from Cedar Summit Farm in New Prague, Minnesota, since around 2005. The bakery highly values Cedar Summit's commitment to 100% grass-fed practices, which results in high-butterfat (around 4.5%) cream-top milk essential for rich pastries. The butter sourced from Cedar Summit is particularly critical, boasting a butterfat content exceeding 84%. This specific high butterfat percentage is a requirement specified by Prairie Hearth to achieve the desired flaky texture and rich flavor in their laminated pastries like croissants and scones. Cedar Summit ensures maximum freshness by delivering dairy products directly to Prairie Hearth's main Minneapolis location twice weekly, typically on Tuesdays and Fridays.

## Bare Honey: Local Flavor Profiles

For specific flavor profiles, particularly in products like their Honey Whole Wheat loaf, Prairie Hearth sources single-origin honey varieties such as Basswood and Wildflower from Minneapolis-based Bare Honey. The bakery typically contracts for around 500 pounds per year to ensure consistency in the distinct flavors these local honeys provide.

## The Nature of Partnership: Beyond the Transaction

Prairie Hearth's relationships with its key suppliers are characterized by transparency and collaboration. The bakery prominently features the names and locations of partners like Meadowlark Organics, Hazzard Free Farm, and Cedar Summit Farm on product labels for signature breads and within their store signage.

Furthermore, Prairie Hearth bakers conduct annual visits to key suppliers, often coinciding with harvest times. These visits, for example to Hazzard Free Farm, allow bakers to directly assess grain quality factors like moisture content and protein levels, discuss specific milling requirements for the upcoming year, and strengthen personal connections. Pre-season contracts, like those with Meadowlark Organics, provide crucial financial stability for farm partners while securing vital ingredients for the bakery. These deep, collaborative relationships are fundamental to Prairie Hearth's ability to produce exceptional baked goods while actively supporting sustainable regional agriculture.
