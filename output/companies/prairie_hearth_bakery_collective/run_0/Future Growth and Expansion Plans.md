# Prairie Hearth Bakery Collective: Future Growth and Expansion Plans

Building on its two-decade foundation of cooperative principles, artisanal baking, and community engagement, Prairie Hearth Bakery Collective is embarking on several strategic initiatives aimed at future growth and expanding its impact. These plans focus on enhancing training opportunities, developing new product lines, strengthening regional partnerships, and exploring innovative models for cooperative expansion.

## Expanding the Apprenticeship Program

A key area of focus is the growth of Prairie Hearth's established apprenticeship program. The collective aims to double the program's capacity, increasing the number of available slots from the current four to eight apprentices per year by 2026. To support this expansion, Prairie Hearth is actively seeking grant funding from regional workforce development initiatives.

Furthermore, the apprenticeship curriculum itself is undergoing enhancement. Planned for implementation in 2025, the updated curriculum will feature a specialized track dedicated to cooperative management and finance. This addition aims to equip graduates with the skills necessary for leadership roles, either within Prairie Hearth or in future cooperative ventures they might pursue.

## Developing New Product Lines

Prairie Hearth is actively working to bring its popular recipes to home bakers through new product lines.

### Packaged Baking Mixes
Development is underway for a line of packaged baking mixes, with an initial focus on adapting the beloved 'North Star Sourdough' and 'Honey Whole Wheat' recipes. Market research conducted in early 2024 also highlighted strong customer demand for a 'Wild Rice & Cranberry Loaf' mix, prompting its inclusion in the first phase of development.

These mixes will feature the same high-quality, pre-measured heirloom flours used in the bakery, such as Red Fife from Meadowlark Organics and Turkey Red wheat, sourced directly from Prairie Hearth's established farm partners in Minnesota and Wisconsin. A pilot launch for these baking mixes is projected for the second quarter of 2025, initially targeting Minneapolis-based food co-ops.

### Frozen Bake-at-Home Goods
Beyond dry mixes, the worker-owners are exploring the potential for a frozen, bake-at-home line. This could feature popular items like Prairie Hearth's signature scones and croissants, offering another convenient way for customers to enjoy fresh-baked goods. A potential launch for this line is targeted for late 2025 or early 2026.

## Strengthening the Supply Chain

To support both the expanded bread production and the new baking mix line, Prairie Hearth recognizes the need to secure a larger supply of high-quality ingredients. The collective plans to establish formal purchasing agreements with at least three additional Minnesota or Wisconsin farms specializing in organic heirloom grains by the end of 2025. This proactive measure ensures sufficient supply while deepening the bakery's commitment to regional agriculture.

## Exploring Cooperative Expansion

Prairie Hearth is investigating innovative ways to broaden its cooperative model. A feasibility study is being conducted throughout 2024-2025 to explore a 'cooperative conversion' model. This concept involves potentially assisting existing independent bakeries in transitioning to a worker-cooperative structure, utilizing the established Prairie Hearth brand and operational principles.

Preliminary market analysis has identified potential locations for this expansion model. Initial discussions have centered on mid-sized Midwestern cities known for their active local food scenes, such as Madison, Wisconsin, and Duluth, Minnesota.

These future plans reflect Prairie Hearth's ongoing commitment to its founding principles: providing high-quality baked goods, fostering democratic workplaces, and strengthening the regional food system through sustainable growth and innovation.
