# Cultivating Skills and Community: Prairie Hearth's Educational Outreach

Prairie Hearth Bakery Collective extends its mission beyond crafting exceptional bread and pastries into the realm of education, fostering both community engagement and workforce development through a variety of programs. From welcoming the public into their baking world to providing intensive skills training, education is a core component of the cooperative's identity.

## Public Baking Workshops: Sharing the Craft

Prairie Hearth regularly opens its doors for public baking workshops, offering hands-on learning experiences at its main Minneapolis facility. These workshops provide participants with practical skills and insights into the bakery's artisanal methods and philosophy.

Popular quarterly offerings cater to diverse interests and seasonal availability. Each winter quarter (January-March), the "Introduction to Sourdough Maintenance and Mixing" workshop guides participants through the fundamentals of working with natural leaven. Come summer, typically in July or August, the "Summer Fruit Pies and Galettes" class utilizes fresh, seasonal produce sourced from local farms. During the spring quarter, experienced baker and worker-owner Miguel Hernandez, alongside founder Sarah Chen, often leads the sought-after "Artisan Bread Shaping Techniques" workshop.

Participation typically costs between $75 and $95, covering all necessary ingredients and the baked goods participants create to take home. Registration is managed online through the bakery's website and opens one month prior to the class date. Beyond the hands-on experience and finished products, attendees receive valuable take-home materials, including a printed recipe booklet and, for relevant classes, a portion of Prairie Hearth's cherished sourdough starter, "Goldie."

## Partnership for Employment: Basic Baking and Food Service Skills

In collaboration with the Cedar-Riverside Employment Center in Minneapolis, Prairie Hearth delivers a targeted 6-week basic baking and food service skills training program. Offered twice annually, this initiative is specifically designed to support local residents facing barriers to employment, equipping them with foundational knowledge for the food industry.

The curriculum covers essential topics crucial for entry-level positions:
*   ServSafe food handling principles
*   Accurate ingredient scaling and measurement techniques
*   Basic bread mixing and shaping methods
*   Introductory customer service protocols

This partnership underscores Prairie Hearth's commitment to community development and creating pathways to meaningful work.

## Formal Apprenticeship Program: Developing Future Bakers and Co-op Leaders

Prairie Hearth runs a comprehensive 18-month formal apprenticeship program, designed to cultivate the next generation of skilled artisanal bakers and potential worker-owners. Apprentices receive structured mentorship and dedicated training modules covering both advanced baking skills and the principles of cooperative management.

Compensation for apprentices begins at $18 per hour. The program seeks individuals passionate about both high-quality baking and the cooperative business model. The application process reflects this dual focus, requiring candidates to submit:
*   A resume
*   A detailed cover letter expressing interest in artisanal baking and the cooperative model
*   Participation in a practical baking trial
*   An interview conducted by the cooperative's Membership Committee

Prairie Hearth is actively working to expand this impactful program. In early 2024, the collective submitted a $50,000 grant proposal to the Minnesota Department of Employment and Economic Development (DEED). The funding aims to support the necessary infrastructure and staffing to double the program's capacity from 4 to 8 apprenticeship slots by 2026.

## Dedicated Oversight: The Education Committee

The quality and relevance of Prairie Hearth's educational offerings are maintained through dedicated oversight. A standing Education Committee, composed of three worker-owners selected for their baking expertise and training experience, manages the curriculum development and annual review processes for both the public workshops and the internal apprenticeship program. This ensures that all educational initiatives remain aligned with the bakery's standards and evolving needs.

Through these diverse educational efforts, Prairie Hearth Bakery Collective actively invests in its community, sharing its passion for baking, fostering essential job skills, and nurturing the principles of cooperation.
