# Prairie Hearth's Commitment: Sustainable Sourcing and a Defined Regional Focus

Prairie Hearth Bakery Collective's dedication to quality and community extends deeply into its sourcing practices. A cornerstone of this commitment is a clearly defined regional focus coupled with stringent sustainability criteria that go beyond standard certifications. This approach not only supports local agriculture but also minimizes environmental impact and ensures transparency for customers.

## The 150-Mile Radius: Defining "Local"

Since its founding principles emphasized regional connections, Prairie Hearth formalized its commitment in 2010 through a General Assembly vote. This established a primary sourcing region defined as a **150-mile radius extending from its main Minneapolis facility**. This policy tightened the initial, less defined "regional" focus from 2004, providing clear geographic boundaries for sourcing efforts.

Within this radius lie key partners essential to Prairie Hearth's operations. Examples include Cedar Summit Farm, located approximately 40 miles south in New Prague, MN, which supplies 100% of the bakery's dairy needs, and Meadowlark Organics, about 140 miles east in Ridgeway, WI, a crucial source for heirloom grains like Red Fife wheat.

## Beyond Organic: Rigorous Sustainability Standards

Prairie Hearth's commitment to sustainability transcends basic USDA Organic certification. The cooperative prioritizes partnerships with farms demonstrating advanced stewardship practices. These include:

*   **Cover Cropping:** Protecting and enriching soil between cash crop cycles.
*   **Reduced Tillage:** Minimizing soil disturbance to improve structure and health.
*   **Integrated Pest Management:** Using ecological approaches to manage pests, reducing reliance on synthetic inputs.
*   **On-Farm Biodiversity:** Promoting a variety of plant and animal life to create resilient agricultural ecosystems.

Verification is key. The **Farm Relations Committee**, composed of worker-owners, conducts **annual site visits** to partner farms to observe and confirm these practices. For farms within the 150-mile radius that are not certified organic but claim sustainable practices, Prairie Hearth employs a detailed internal questionnaire covering soil health, water usage, and labor practices. This is supplemented by mandatory annual farm visits before any purchasing agreement is established.

## Measurable Impact: Local Sourcing Achievements

Prairie Hearth actively tracks its regional sourcing as a key performance indicator (KPI) in its annual sustainability reporting. This focus aims to minimize food miles and the associated transportation emissions. As of 2024, the collective has achieved significant milestones:

*   Approximately **80% of total flour volume** is sourced locally from Minnesota and Wisconsin.
*   Nearly **100% of whole grain flours** (including rye, spelt, Red Fife, and Turkey Red) come from within this region.
*   A significant portion of their base organic bread flour is also sourced locally.
*   At least **90% of perishable ingredients**, such as dairy from Cedar Summit Farm, eggs, and seasonal produce, originate from farms strictly within the 150-mile radius.

## Operational Integration and Resilience

The regional sourcing strategy is deeply embedded in Prairie Hearth's operations:

*   **Seasonal Menu Planning:** The development calendar for seasonal pastries and specialty breads is directly linked to the harvest schedules of partner farms. Bakers plan menus 3-6 months in advance based on anticipated availability, featuring items like Wisconsin cranberries or Minnesota rhubarb when they are in season.
*   **Supply Chain Resilience:** To mitigate risks like single-supplier crop failures, Prairie Hearth actively seeks secondary suppliers for critical ingredients, such as organic bread flour and key heirloom grains, specifically within the 150-mile radius.

## Transparency and Connection

Prairie Hearth believes in transparency regarding its sourcing. The bakery's website features an **interactive map** showcasing the locations of its primary farm partners within the 150-mile radius. This tool reinforces the bakery's local sourcing commitments and allows customers to see the direct connection between the bakery and the farms supplying its ingredients.

By adhering to a defined 150-mile radius and rigorous sustainability criteria, Prairie Hearth Bakery Collective demonstrates a profound commitment to strengthening the regional food system, supporting sustainable agriculture, and providing its community with high-quality baked goods rooted in local partnerships.
