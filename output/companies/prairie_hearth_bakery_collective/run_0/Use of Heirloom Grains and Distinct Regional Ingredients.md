# The Foundation of Flavor: Heirloom Grains and Regional Ingredients at Prairie Hearth

Prairie Hearth Bakery Collective's commitment to crafting exceptional artisanal breads and pastries is deeply rooted in its meticulous selection of ingredients. Central to their philosophy is the use of specific heirloom grains and distinct regional products, reflecting a dedication not only to superior flavor and quality but also to supporting local agriculture and sustainable practices throughout the Midwest.

## Celebrating Heritage Grains

The bakery actively seeks out heritage and ancient grains known for their unique characteristics, moving beyond commodity flour. Several key grains form the backbone of their offerings:

*   **Turkey Red Wheat:** A favored choice, Turkey Red is renowned for its rich, complex flavor profile featuring nutty undertones. Its excellent baking properties contribute significantly to the deep color and satisfying taste of Prairie Hearth's whole wheat loaves.
*   **Red Fife Wheat:** Sourced from partners like Meadowlark Organics in Wisconsin, Red Fife is prized by the bakers for its subtly sweet, almost cinnamon-like notes. Its strong gluten structure makes it particularly well-suited for naturally leavened hearth breads.
*   **Spelt:** An ancient relative of wheat, spelt finds its way into specialty loaves. It offers a distinct nutty and slightly sweet flavor. Compared to modern wheat, its lower gluten strength results in a more tender crumb in the final product.

Prairie Hearth specifies stone-milled flour from partner farms like Hazzard Free Farm whenever possible. They believe this traditional milling process retains more of the grain's natural oils, nutrients, and inherent flavor compared to industrial roller milling, contributing significantly to the final product's quality. This commitment is reflected in their sourcing achievements; as of 2024, approximately 80% of their total flour volume comes directly from Minnesota and Wisconsin farms, exceeding their initial goals.

## A Taste of the Region

Beyond grains, Prairie Hearth showcases the bounty of the region through carefully sourced ingredients:

*   **The 'Wild Rice & Cranberry Loaf':** This signature product exemplifies regional sourcing. The hand-harvested wild rice is primarily sourced through partnerships supporting indigenous harvesters in Northern Minnesota's lake regions, such as near Leech Lake. Making up approximately 15% of the formula relative to flour weight, the cooked wild rice adds a distinctive chewy texture and nutty flavor profile unique to the area. Providing a tart counterpoint are dried, unsweetened cranberries, typically sourced from growers near Warrens, Wisconsin – the "Cranberry Capital."
*   **Seasonal Specialties:** The bakery's offerings change with the seasons. Fall pastries frequently feature specific regional apple varieties like SweeTango® and First Kiss®, sourced from orchards in the Lake Pepin region straddling Minnesota and Wisconsin. Autumn breads might incorporate Wisconsin Black Walnuts, while specific pastry glazes benefit from the rich flavor of Minnesota-grown maple syrup sourced from Sapsucker Farms in Mora, MN. Local honey from Minneapolis-based Bare Honey also finds its way into certain recipes.

## Innovation and Sustainable Sourcing

Prairie Hearth's commitment extends to exploring innovative and sustainable ingredients. They incorporate Minnesota-grown Kernza®, a perennial grain developed by The Land Institute and researched at the University of Minnesota, into experimental batches. Kernza® is particularly valued for its deep root system, which offers significant soil health benefits. This aligns with the bakery's broader mission, which includes prioritizing partnerships with farms employing certified organic or sustainable agricultural practices and conducting annual visits to strengthen these relationships.

In conclusion, Prairie Hearth Bakery Collective demonstrates that exceptional baking starts with exceptional ingredients. Their deliberate choice of heirloom grains like Turkey Red, Red Fife, and Spelt, combined with unique regional elements such as Northern Minnesota wild rice, Wisconsin cranberries, and Lake Pepin apples, defines the character of their breads and pastries. This thoughtful sourcing strategy not only creates distinctive flavors and textures but also reinforces their commitment to the regional food system and sustainable agriculture.
