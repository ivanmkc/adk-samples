# Fractional Ownership at Artisan Digital Collective: Technology and Governance

Artisan Digital Collective (ADC), known for its curated approach to high-value digital art and its proprietary **Artisan Authenticator** NFTs, is actively developing fractional ownership models. This initiative aligns with ADC's mission while exploring new avenues for collector engagement with significant digital artworks, leveraging sophisticated technology and carefully structured governance protocols.

## Technological Foundation: The 'Fractional Authenticator'

At the heart of ADC's fractional ownership system is a custom implementation based on the ERC-1155 multi-token standard, internally codenamed the **'Fractional Authenticator'**. Unlike standard NFTs (like ADC's parent **Artisan Authenticators** which represent whole ownership), this allows a single high-value artwork's ownership to be divided into multiple distinct, tradable fractions.

A key feature is that each **Fractional Authenticator** token is designed to inherit and display specific subsets of metadata from the parent **Artisan Authenticator**. This includes crucial information such as immutable provenance links and detailed exhibition history, ensuring that fractional owners retain access to the core authentication data associated with the artwork. The underlying logic is implemented via dedicated suites of Solidity smart contracts.

## Governance Framework: Empowering Collective Ownership

ADC is piloting a **quadratic voting** mechanism to empower fractional owners in collective decision-making. This system, implemented via smart contracts audited by the reputable firm **ConsenSys Diligence**, allows owners to vote on proposals concerning the co-owned artwork. Proposed uses include voting on exhibition loan approvals or setting a reserve price for potential future sales.

To ensure meaningful participation, a minimum quorum representing 30% of the total fraction value is required to initiate a binding vote. While **quadratic voting** aims to distribute influence more equitably, the governing smart contracts for the pilot program grant ADC, as the platform operator, specific administrative rights. This includes a potential veto on sale proposals falling below a pre-agreed reserve price, designed to protect the artwork's long-term value and the interests of the original artist.

## Privacy Enhancements: zk-SNARKs in Governance

Recognizing the importance of privacy for its clientele, ADC plans to leverage **zk-SNARKs** (Zero-Knowledge Succinct Non-Interactive Arguments of Knowledge) within the fractional ownership governance system. This advanced cryptographic technique will enable owners to participate in **quadratic voting** proposals and prove their voting rights without publicly revealing the precise value or percentage of their individual shareholdings, enhancing discretion.

## Legal and Regulatory Strategy: Navigating Compliance

ADC is taking a meticulous approach to the legal and regulatory complexities of fractional ownership, particularly focusing on Swiss jurisdiction. The collective is engaged in legal consultations in Zug, Switzerland (often referred to as 'Crypto Valley'), specifically structuring its offerings to comply with the Swiss Collective Investment Schemes Act (**CISA**) and the Financial Market Supervisory Authority's (**FINMA**) anti-money laundering (**AML**) ordinances.

This strategy likely involves targeting qualified investors under Swiss law, streamlining regulatory requirements. To manage these complexities effectively, ADC is establishing a dedicated Swiss subsidiary, structured as a **GmbH** (Gesellschaft mit beschränkter Haftung). This entity will be specifically tasked with issuing **Fractional Authenticators**, managing the collective owner governance protocols, and ensuring ongoing compliance with **FINMA** regulations.

## Pilot Program: High-Value Generative Art

The initial fractional ownership pilot is rumored to feature a high-value generative artwork by renowned artist Refik Anadol, specifically 'Machine Hallucination – Latent Study 12'. This piece is reportedly valued at over $1.5 million USD. ADC plans to divide ownership of this work into 100 distinct **Fractional Authenticator** tokens.

Reflecting ADC's continued focus on accredited investors and high-net-worth clientele, the projected minimum investment threshold to acquire a single fraction in this pilot offering is set at $15,000 USD.

## Platform Integration and User Experience

Fractional ownership is being seamlessly integrated into ADC's existing high-fidelity platform. Within the Unreal Engine 5 VR environment, verified owners of **Fractional Authenticators** will be distinguished by a unique visual emblem displayed on their avatar profile.

Furthermore, owners will gain access to a dedicated, secure **'Fractional Owners Lounge'** within the VR platform. This exclusive virtual space will serve as a hub for viewing governance proposals, participating in related discussions with fellow owners, and tracking the outcomes of votes concerning their co-owned artwork.

## Regulated Secondary Market and Artist Royalties

ADC intends to create and regulate an exclusive secondary market for trading **Fractional Authenticators** directly within its platform. This aims to provide liquidity for fractional owners within a controlled and compliant environment. Participants in this secondary market will be required to pass ADC's standard Know Your Customer (KYC) and Anti-Money Laundering (AML) verification processes.

Crucially, the secondary market mechanism will incorporate automated smart contract functions designed to benefit the original artists. Upon each verified trade of a fraction, these functions will automatically calculate and distribute artist royalties, potentially set between 8% and 12% of the transaction value, ensuring artists continue to participate in the ongoing value creation of their work.

## Conclusion

ADC's foray into fractional ownership represents a sophisticated blend of cutting-edge blockchain technology (custom ERC-1155, Solidity, **zk-SNARKs**), structured governance (**quadratic voting**, quorum rules), careful regulatory planning (Swiss **CISA**/**FINMA** compliance), and seamless integration into its exclusive VR platform. By focusing on high-value art, accredited investors, and robust technological and legal frameworks, ADC aims to establish a new benchmark for collective ownership in the digital art market.
