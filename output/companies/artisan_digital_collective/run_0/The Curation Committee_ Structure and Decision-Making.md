# The ADC Curation Committee: Structure and Decision-Making

Central to the Artisan Digital Collective's (ADC) strategy of extreme curation and maintaining high barriers to entry is its Curation Committee. This body serves as the ultimate gatekeeper, ensuring that only artists meeting the collective's stringent standards are accepted onto the platform. Its structure and processes are designed for rigorous evaluation, confidentiality, and alignment with ADC's mission to elevate digital art.

## Committee Composition and Tenure

The Curation Committee is composed of five members and is personally chaired by ADC co-founder Seraphina Dubois. Alongside the Chair, the committee includes distinguished figures from the art world. Notable members include Dr. Aris Thorne, a respected digital art historian affiliated with the University of Basel, and the influential generative artist known only by the pseudonym 'Cipher'.

To ensure a blend of consistent oversight and evolving perspectives, committee members (excluding the permanent Chair) serve staggered three-year terms. There is a limit of two consecutive terms for each member, facilitating the periodic introduction of new viewpoints while maintaining institutional knowledge.

## Meeting Cadence and Secure Deliberations

The committee convenes quarterly for intensive two-day virtual sessions. These meetings are typically scheduled during the first weeks of March, June, September, and December. Reflecting the sensitive nature of the discussions, all sessions are conducted using ADC's proprietary secure communication platform, ensuring the confidentiality of the proceedings.

## Rigorous Evaluation Process

During each quarterly meeting cycle, the Curation Committee undertakes a meticulous evaluation of artist candidates who have successfully navigated ADC's preliminary vetting stages. On average, between 15 and 20 pre-vetted candidates, identified through ADC's exclusive scouting and referral network, reach this final review stage.

For every candidate artist, the committee examines a comprehensive dossier. This package includes:
*   A curated portfolio showcasing the artist's work.
*   A technical validation report from ADC's internal experts assessing digital methods.
*   Extensive notes compiled by the scouting team or the referring party.
*   A summary of the preliminary interview conducted by Seraphina Dubois.

## Unanimous Decision-Making and Integrity

A defining characteristic of the committee's process is the requirement for unanimous agreement for any artist to be selected. This high threshold underscores ADC's commitment to exceptional quality and shared vision. The formal decision is recorded when all five committee members submit individual, cryptographically signed digital approvals through a dedicated internal voting module integrated into the meeting platform.

To maintain impartiality, the committee adheres to a formal conflict of interest policy. Any member who has a significant prior professional or personal relationship with a candidate artist, including being the source of their direct referral, must declare the conflict. Such members are required to recuse themselves from the final vote concerning that specific candidate.

If initial unanimity is not reached during the review, the candidate artist is generally not reconsidered. An exception is made only if substantial new supporting information emerges before the subsequent meeting. Otherwise, the lack of consensus effectively serves as a final rejection.

## Strict Confidentiality

The integrity and confidentiality of the committee's work are paramount. All members are bound by extensive multi-year non-disclosure agreements (NDAs). These agreements strictly prohibit the revelation of any details regarding committee deliberations, the identities of candidate artists (both accepted and rejected), internal debates, or the specific reasons behind any rejection.

## Impact on ADC Strategy

Following the successful selection of an artist, the Curation Committee provides a concise summary rationale outlining the reasons for their acceptance. This rationale is a crucial internal document that informs the strategic positioning and initial promotional narrative developed by ADC's marketing and client relations teams, ensuring alignment between the curatorial vision and market presentation.

In conclusion, the ADC Curation Committee operates through a highly structured, confidential, and rigorous process. Its composition, meeting protocols, evaluation methods, and unanimous decision requirement collectively function to uphold the exclusivity and critical standards that define the Artisan Digital Collective in the digital art landscape.
