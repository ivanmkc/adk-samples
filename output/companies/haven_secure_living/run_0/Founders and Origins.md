# Founders and Origins of Haven Secure Living

Haven Secure Living stands at the intersection of advanced technology and proactive security, a vision brought to life by its two distinct co-founders: Alistair Finch and Dr. Evelyn Hayes. Their unique backgrounds and shared perception of evolving threats laid the groundwork for the company's innovative approach.

## The Visionaries

Alistair Finch brought extensive real-world operational experience to the partnership. He served for 12 years in the prestigious British Special Air Service (SAS), where he specialized in counter-terrorism, cyber warfare, and intelligence gathering. This background instilled a deep understanding of threat anticipation and neutralization.

Dr. Evelyn Hayes provided the technological ingenuity. Holding a Ph.D. in Robotics and Artificial Intelligence from the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), her research focused specifically on swarm intelligence and autonomous navigation systems, areas critical to developing advanced, coordinated security solutions.

## A Meeting of Minds

The paths of Finch and Hayes converged in 2015 at a NATO cybersecurity summit held in Brussels, Belgium. Dr. Hayes was presenting her research on the security of autonomous systems, capturing Finch's attention. The true catalyst for their venture, however, emerged a year later. In 2016, Finch consulted on a major corporate data breach, an experience that starkly revealed the limitations of traditional, reactive security measures against sophisticated, modern threats.

## Forging a New Paradigm

Recognizing a critical gap in the market, Finch and Hayes envisioned a unified platform capable of addressing the convergence of physical and digital threats. They believed existing security solutions were inadequate for the challenges of the mid-2010s. Their founding mission was clear: to integrate cutting-edge AI, robotics, and cybersecurity principles to create a truly preemptive security paradigm, especially for high-value targets.

Driven by this vision, Haven Secure Living was officially incorporated in Delaware in early 2017. The nascent company secured initial seed funding primarily from private investors, many with backgrounds in the defense technology sector, who recognized the potential of their approach.

## Shaping the Technology and Philosophy

The founders' respective expertise directly shaped Haven Secure Living's core tenets and technologies. Alistair Finch's military experience heavily influenced the company's proactive security philosophy, emphasizing threat anticipation and neutralization *before* an incident could occur, a departure from reactive models.

Dr. Hayes' doctoral work on multi-agent robotic systems provided the foundational algorithms that would power the coordinated patrol patterns used by the company's Sentinel drones. Furthermore, an early, pre-incorporation project led jointly by Finch and Hayes focused on developing predictive behavioral analysis algorithms. This foundational work later evolved into the core intelligence of the Guardian AI, a key component of their security platform.

Together, Finch and Hayes established Haven Secure Living not just as a company, but as the embodiment of a forward-thinking security philosophy built on anticipation, integration, and technological superiority.
