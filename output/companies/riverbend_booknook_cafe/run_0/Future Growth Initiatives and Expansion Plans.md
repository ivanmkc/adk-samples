# Riverbend BookNook & Café: Charting a Course for Growth (2024-2029)

Riverbend BookNook & Café is embarking on a significant phase of development, outlining ambitious plans for expansion and diversification over the next five years. Building on its established identity as a community-focused "third place," the company is poised to extend its reach geographically and explore new avenues for engaging with readers and promoting Southern culture.

## Expanding the Footprint: New Store Locations

A cornerstone of the growth strategy is a five-year expansion plan spanning 2024 to 2029. Riverbend aims to open between 8 and 10 new locations during this period. The primary focus for this expansion will be underserved college towns and state capitals within North Carolina, South Carolina, and Tennessee.

Site selection for these new stores follows specific criteria designed to replicate the success of existing locations. Riverbend prioritizes downtown or main street districts characterized by high foot traffic and proximity to universities or cultural centers. Potential buildings must offer at least 2,500 square feet to accommodate both the bookstore and café elements.

To effectively manage this geographical growth, Riverbend plans to establish two new regional manager positions by early 2025. Each manager will oversee a cluster of 5 to 7 stores, ensuring operational consistency and localized support.

## Diversification: Subscription Boxes and Publishing

Beyond physical stores, Riverbend is venturing into new service models. A planned subscription box service, tentatively named "Riverbend Reads & Roasts," is scheduled for a pilot launch in the fourth quarter of 2024. Initially, this service will be available exclusively to customers in Georgia and South Carolina. Each quarterly box is envisioned to contain a signed edition from a Southern author, a 12oz bag of coffee from a featured Southeastern micro-roaster, and one or two artisanal goods sourced from the author's home state, further emphasizing the regional focus.

Furthermore, the company is exploring the possibility of launching its own publishing imprint, potentially called "Riverbend Press." Co-founder Eleanor Ainsworth is currently leading a feasibility study, with a decision expected by mid-2025. If established, Riverbend Press aims to publish 4 to 6 titles annually, concentrating initially on debut novels by Southern writers and regional history monographs.

## Deepening Community Ties: School Partnerships

Continuing its commitment to community engagement, Riverbend is exploring potential partnerships with local schools. The model under consideration involves Riverbend hosting after-school book clubs at designated Title I elementary schools. This initiative would include providing the necessary books and staff facilitators, directly supporting literacy programs in underserved communities.

## Infrastructure for Growth: Funding and Technology

Supporting these ambitious initiatives requires significant investment in infrastructure. Riverbend plans a major website overhaul in 2025. This upgrade will introduce e-commerce capabilities essential for managing the "Riverbend Reads & Roasts" subscription box, selling potential Riverbend Press titles, and offering curated book bundles online.

To finance the initial phase of store expansion and the launch infrastructure for the subscription box service, the company is in the process of securing a dedicated line of credit, estimated at $1.5 million. This funding will be crucial in realizing the outlined growth plans over the coming years.

Through strategic expansion, innovative service offerings, and continued community focus, Riverbend BookNook & Café is positioning itself for substantial growth while remaining true to its core mission of fostering literary culture and community connection across the Southeast.
