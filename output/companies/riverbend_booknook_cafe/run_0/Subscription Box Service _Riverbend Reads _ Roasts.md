# Riverbend Reads & Roasts: Subscription Box Pilot Launch

Riverbend BookNook & Café is preparing to launch its anticipated subscription box service, tentatively named 'Riverbend Reads & Roasts'. A pilot program is scheduled to begin in the fourth quarter of 2024.

## Pilot Program Overview

The initial pilot launch aims to establish a foundational subscriber base of 250 to 300 customers. This phase will focus exclusively on residents within the states of Georgia and South Carolina. During the pilot, the introductory quarterly price for the 'Riverbend Reads & Roasts' box is set at $49.95. Subscribers will also be responsible for state-applicable sales tax and a flat-rate shipping fee of $7.50 per box.

## Curation and Contents

Each box's contents are carefully selected by a dedicated three-person curation team. This team is led by Riverbend co-founder Eleanor Ainsworth and includes a senior book buyer and the lead café operations manager.

The box will feature a 12oz bag of coffee, rotating quarterly among pre-vetted Southeastern micro-roasters already supplying Riverbend cafés, such as 'Coastal Plains Coffee Roasters'. The specific roaster chosen each quarter will aim to complement the featured author's region or the box's seasonal theme.

In addition to the coffee and featured author focus, each box will contain 1-2 artisanal goods. These items must be produced by small businesses located within the featured author's home state. The curation team prioritizes non-perishable food items (like jams, spices, or locally made snacks) or literary-themed crafts, sourced through partnerships they establish.

## Operations and Packaging

Fulfillment operations for the pilot program will be centralized within a dedicated 500 sq ft space located in the back-of-house area of the flagship store in Savannah, Georgia.

Significant attention is being paid to the packaging. Each 'Riverbend Reads & Roasts' box will utilize custom-designed, eco-friendly materials. The packaging will feature the Riverbend logo and artwork commissioned specifically from a Savannah College of Art and Design (SCAD) alumnus. Environmentally conscious materials, including biodegradable packing peanuts, will be used.

Looking ahead, Riverbend plans to manage the subscription service using the 'Subbly' subscription management platform. This platform will be integrated into the company's overhauled website, planned for 2025, to handle recurring billing, customer accounts, and shipping logistics.

## Marketing and Future Expansion

Initial marketing efforts for the pilot program will heavily target existing Riverbend loyalty program members residing in Georgia and South Carolina. This outreach will primarily occur through email campaigns and in-store promotional displays. An incentive is planned for early adopters: a 10% discount on the first box for customers who sign up before November 1st, 2024.

The future expansion of 'Riverbend Reads & Roasts' is contingent on the success of the pilot program. Success is defined as achieving over 80% subscriber retention after the first two quarterly cycles. If this benchmark is met, Riverbend plans to expand the service's availability to customers in North Carolina and Tennessee by the third quarter of 2025.
