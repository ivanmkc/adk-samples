# Riverbend BookNook & Café: Expansion Strategy and Site Selection Criteria

Riverbend BookNook & Café is embarking on a strategic expansion phase, aiming to bring its unique blend of bookstore and café – its signature 'third place' environment – to new communities across the Southeastern United States. The company has outlined a clear set of criteria and a meticulous process for identifying and selecting new locations, particularly focusing on North Carolina, South Carolina, and Tennessee for the 2024-2029 period, with a goal of opening 8-10 new stores.

## Identifying Target Communities

Riverbend's approach begins with identifying promising towns that fit a specific profile:

*   **Population and Status:** The primary search focuses on towns with populations ranging from 30,000 to 75,000 residents. Critically, these towns must also host either a four-year university or serve as a state capital, indicating a potential base of engaged readers and community members.
*   **Demographics:** Beyond population size, Riverbend conducts detailed demographic analysis. Target towns must demonstrate that over 30% of their residents hold at least a bachelor's degree. Furthermore, the median household income should fall within the $55,000 to $85,000 range.
*   **Market Niche:** A key criterion is that the potential location is currently underserved. Riverbend specifically defines "lacking an independent bookstore" as having no existing general-interest independent bookstore primarily selling new books within a 15-mile radius of the proposed downtown or main street site.

Based on these criteria, potential locations actively researched or visited during the 2024-2025 period include cultural hubs and university towns such as Asheville, North Carolina; Columbia, South Carolina; and Knoxville, Tennessee.

## The Site Evaluation Process

Once a potential town is identified, Riverbend employs a multi-stage evaluation process for specific sites:

*   **Market Validation:** Before making significant commitments, Riverbend often conducts low-cost market validation tests. This might involve operating a pop-up bookstall at a local farmers' market or festival for one or two weekends to gauge local interest and reception.
*   **On-Site Evaluation:** A mandatory 2-day on-site evaluation visit is conducted by a senior operations manager. This visit utilizes a standardized 50-point checklist to rigorously assess various factors. Key checklist items include pedestrian traffic counts at different times of day, proximity and availability of parking, the physical condition of the potential building, and the presence of nearby 'third place' anchors like public parks or libraries.
*   **Ecosystem Assessment:** The checklist also includes points for evaluating the health and proximity of complementary independent businesses. The presence of thriving non-chain restaurants, art galleries, craft breweries, and independent cinemas is viewed as a positive indicator of a vibrant local commercial ecosystem that aligns with Riverbend's community focus.

## Building and Lease Preferences

Riverbend has specific preferences for the physical spaces it occupies:

*   **Building Character:** There is a strong preference for leasing existing buildings constructed before 1960. The company seeks structures with architectural character that are suitable for renovation, contributing to the unique atmosphere of each location.
*   **Size and Location:** Sites are typically sought in downtown or main street districts with high foot traffic, ideally near universities or cultural centers. A minimum of 2,500 square feet is generally required to accommodate both the bookstore and café elements comfortably.
*   **Lease Terms:** Riverbend typically negotiates initial lease terms of 5 to 7 years, ensuring stability, and includes options for renewal.

## Financial Viability and Final Approval

Before any lease is signed, rigorous financial and committee reviews are required:

*   **Financial Assessment:** A potential new location must pass a detailed financial viability assessment. This assessment must project that the store can reach its break-even point within 18 months of opening. Furthermore, it must demonstrate a minimum projected Return on Investment (ROI) of 12% by the third year of operation.
*   **Committee Approval:** Final approval for securing a new store location rests with a dedicated site selection committee. This committee requires unanimous agreement to proceed. Its members include co-founders Eleanor and Thomas Ainsworth, the Chief Financial Officer, and the individual designated as the future Regional Manager for the area, ensuring alignment across leadership, finance, and future operations.

Through this comprehensive and criteria-driven process, Riverbend BookNook & Café aims to ensure that each new location has the optimal conditions for success, allowing it to become a valued 'third place' within its chosen community.
