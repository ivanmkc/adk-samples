# Sustainable Manufacturing Infrastructure at the Portland Facility

Ember Automotive's commitment to "Responsible Artistry" extends deeply into its manufacturing operations, exemplified by the state-of-the-art, sustainable infrastructure at its headquarters and production facility in Portland, Oregon. This facility, which has achieved the prestigious LEED Platinum certification, was intentionally located in Portland to align with the city's strong sustainability ethos and serves as a physical manifestation of the company's core values.

## Advanced Energy Management

A cornerstone of the facility's energy strategy is a massive 5-acre rooftop solar array. With a peak generating capacity of 2.5 Megawatts (MW), this installation produces approximately 3,000 Megawatt-hours (MWh) of clean electricity annually. This significant output covers over 60% of the factory's total energy requirements.

To address the remaining electricity demand (approximately 40%), Ember Automotive purchases Renewable Energy Certificates (RECs). These certificates are primarily sourced from regional wind power projects located in Oregon and Washington, ensuring that the facility operates on effectively 100% renewable electricity.

Energy efficiency is further enhanced throughout the factory. High-efficiency LED lighting fixtures are installed universally, coupled with smart controls like occupancy sensors and daylight harvesting systems. This integrated approach reduces lighting-related energy consumption by over 50% compared to standard industrial practices. The building envelope itself is designed for minimal energy loss, featuring high R-value insulation (R-30 in walls, R-40 in the roof) and low-emissivity (Low-E) coated glazing. These features significantly reduce thermal transfer, leading to an estimated 25% reduction in HVAC energy requirements.

## Comprehensive Water Conservation

Water usage is meticulously managed through Ember's closed-loop water recycling system. This advanced system treats and reuses over 95% of the water consumed in high-volume manufacturing processes, such as the vehicle paint shop and component washing stations. Compared to conventional manufacturing practices, this system yields substantial savings, estimated at 5 million gallons of municipal water per year.

## Sustainable Materials and Waste Reduction

Sustainability was integrated from the ground up. During construction, over 20% of the building materials (by value) were sourced from recycled content. Notable examples include recycled steel for structural components and the use of fly ash as a partial cement replacement in concrete foundations, contributing significantly to the LEED Platinum certification.

Operationally, the facility runs a comprehensive waste management program that achieves a landfill diversion rate exceeding 90%. This is accomplished through carefully segregated recycling streams for metals, plastics, and composites, alongside an on-site composting system for organic waste. Hazardous waste streams, such as spent solvents or materials from battery testing, are handled with utmost responsibility. These materials are managed through certified third-party providers specializing in environmentally sound disposal and resource recovery, strictly adhering to EPA guidelines.

## Eco-Conscious Site Design

The facility's commitment to the environment extends to the site itself. Three acres of the property are dedicated to bioswales and landscaping featuring native, drought-tolerant plants. This design naturally filters stormwater runoff before it leaves the site and significantly reduces the need for irrigation, contributing valuable credits towards the LEED Sustainable Sites category.

Through these integrated systems – from renewable energy generation and water recycling to waste diversion and sustainable site management – the Portland facility embodies Ember Automotive's dedication to minimizing environmental impact while producing ultra-luxury electric vehicles.
