# Ember Electric Powertrain: Performance and Dynamics

Ember Automotive's vehicles, the Aura sedan and Solstice SUV, are engineered with a focus on high performance, dynamic handling, and efficiency, underpinned by an advanced electric powertrain and sophisticated chassis systems.

## Dual-Motor All-Wheel Drive System

At the heart of both the Ember Aura and Solstice lies a high-efficiency powertrain featuring dual Permanent Magnet Synchronous Motors (PMSM). One motor is dedicated to the front axle and another to the rear axle, creating a responsive and powerful All-Wheel Drive (AWD) configuration. This dual-motor setup enables fully variable torque distribution. The system can instantaneously shift power delivery between the front and rear axles, ranging from 0% to 100% on either axle as conditions demand, ensuring optimal traction and dynamic handling across various driving scenarios.

## Performance Capabilities

The advanced powertrain translates into significant performance figures. The Ember Aura sedan boasts an electronically limited top speed of 180 mph (approximately 290 km/h), while the Ember Solstice SUV achieves an electronically limited top speed of 165 mph (approximately 265 km/h).

## Advanced Driving Dynamics and Suspension

Ember vehicles utilize several technologies to enhance driving dynamics:

*   **Torque Vectoring:** An advanced brake-based torque vectoring system subtly applies braking force to individual wheels during cornering. This action enhances turn-in agility and contributes to greater dynamic stability.
*   **Adaptive Air Suspension:** Both models feature an adaptive air suspension system. This system offers adjustable ride height and continuously variable damping rates, adapting to road conditions and driver preferences. It integrates seamlessly with selectable drive modes – Comfort, Sport, and Efficiency – and also responds to adjustments made by the EmpathAI system.
*   **Solstice 'Trail' Mode:** The Ember Solstice SUV includes an additional 'Trail' drive mode specifically designed for off-pavement capability. Engaging this mode optimizes the AWD torque split for low-grip surfaces, raises the air suspension to provide a maximum ground clearance of 9.5 inches, and adjusts the throttle mapping for finer control in challenging terrain.

## Driver Control and Efficiency

Drivers can tailor the driving experience through adjustable systems:

*   **Regenerative Braking:** Multiple levels of regenerative braking intensity can be selected using steering wheel-mounted paddles or the central touchscreen. This allows drivers to choose preferences ranging from minimal resistance, mimicking traditional coasting, to a strong regenerative effect enabling full one-pedal driving.

## Thermal Management

To ensure consistent performance, especially during demanding driving or fast charging sessions, a sophisticated liquid thermal management system is employed. This system actively regulates the temperature of both the dual electric motors and the solid-state battery pack, maintaining optimal operating conditions.

## Braking Systems

Complementing the powertrain's performance is a capable braking system. For drivers seeking the ultimate stopping power and resistance to brake fade, high-performance Brembo carbon-ceramic disc brakes are available as part of an optional 'Dynamic Performance Package' for both the Aura and Solstice models.
