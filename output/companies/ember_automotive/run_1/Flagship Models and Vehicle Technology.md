# Ember Automotive: Flagship Models and Advanced Technology

Ember Automotive showcases its commitment to ultra-luxury electric mobility and cutting-edge innovation through its flagship models: the Ember Aura sedan and the Ember Solstice SUV. These vehicles integrate groundbreaking powertrain, battery, and autonomous driving technologies.

## Powertrain and Performance

Both the Aura and Solstice are built upon a sophisticated dual-motor, all-wheel-drive configuration.
*   The **Ember Aura sedan** boasts formidable performance, generating a combined output of 850 horsepower and 900 lb-ft of torque.
*   The **Ember Solstice SUV**, utilizing a similar powertrain architecture, delivers rapid acceleration, capable of reaching 0 to 60 mph in just 3.8 seconds.

## Revolutionary Solid-State Battery Technology

At the heart of Ember's vehicles lies advanced solid-state battery technology, offering significant advantages in range and energy density.
*   **Range:** The Ember Aura sedan achieves an impressive EPA-estimated range exceeding 550 miles on a single charge. The slightly larger pack in the Solstice SUV provides an estimated range of 520 miles.
*   **Energy Density:** Ember's proprietary solid-state batteries achieve an energy density greater than 450 Wh/kg. This high density allows for substantial weight savings compared to contemporary lithium-ion cells, contributing directly to the extended range and improved vehicle dynamics.

## Ultra-Fast Charging

Complementing the advanced batteries is a proprietary 800V electrical architecture. This high-voltage system enables remarkably fast charging times. Using compatible DC fast chargers, the solid-state batteries in both the Aura and Solstice can replenish their charge from 10% to 80% capacity in under 15 minutes.

## Level 4/5 Autonomous Driving System

Ember vehicles are equipped with a highly advanced autonomous driving system, designed for Level 4 and Level 5 capabilities.
*   **Sensor Suite:** Robust environmental perception is achieved through a comprehensive sensor suite, including 5 long-range LiDAR units, 12 high-definition cameras, and 6 radar sensors.
*   **Operational Domain:** Initially, the Level 4 autonomous operation is geofenced. It is available on pre-mapped major highways and within specific urban zones located in California and Oregon. Ember plans to expand these operational domains progressively through over-the-air (OTA) software updates.
*   **EmpathAI Integration:** The proprietary EmpathAI system actively influences the autonomous driving experience. It modulates the driving profile by adjusting acceleration and deceleration curves, as well as cornering behavior, based on its inference of passenger comfort levels and preferences, aiming for a smoother, more personalized journey.

## Advanced Comfort and Convenience Features

Beyond performance and autonomy, Ember integrates technology to enhance the cabin environment. Both the Aura and Solstice feature a dynamic electrochromic glass roof. This smart glass can automatically adjust its tint level in response to ambient light conditions or passenger preferences via voice command, seamlessly complementing the EmpathAI system's efforts to create an optimal and adaptive interior atmosphere.
