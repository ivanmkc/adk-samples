# Ember Automotive's Commitment: Weaving Sustainability with Textiles from Recycled Ocean Plastics

Ember Automotive, known for its ultra-luxury electric vehicles and commitment to 'Responsible Artistry,' integrates sustainability deep within its material choices. A prime example is the innovative use of premium textiles derived directly from recycled ocean plastics, transforming marine waste into sophisticated interior components.

## Sourcing Through Strategic Partnership: The SEAQUAL INITIATIVE

To procure these unique textiles, Ember Automotive formalized a partnership in 2021 with the SEAQUAL INITIATIVE. Headquartered in Girona, Catalonia, Spain, this organization spearheads efforts to combat marine pollution. The SEAQUAL INITIATIVE collaborates extensively with fishermen, non-governmental organizations (NGOs), and local communities to collect marine litter. These clean-up operations primarily focus on the Mediterranean Sea and the Atlantic coasts of Europe and Africa.

## From Marine Litter to Luxury Yarn

The journey from ocean waste to automotive textile is a meticulous process. Collected marine litter first undergoes rigorous sorting and cleaning. It is then processed into a material known as UPSEA™ Marine Plastic. This intermediate product is subsequently transformed into Seaqual Yarn, a premium, high-quality filament made entirely from 100% recycled polyester.

While the SEAQUAL INITIATIVE processes various forms of plastic waste recovered from marine environments, including fishing nets, the Seaqual Yarn utilized in Ember vehicles is primarily derived from post-consumer PET plastic bottles.

## Application within Ember Vehicles

Ember Automotive incorporates a significant amount of this sustainable material into each vehicle. Approximately 4 square meters of textile woven from Seaqual Yarn features in the interior of every Ember car. This usage is equivalent to recycling roughly 500 post-consumer plastic bottles recovered from the ocean per vehicle.

Specifically, this durable and aesthetically pleasing fabric is employed for:
*   Vehicle floor mats
*   Trunk lining
*   Seatback map pockets
*   Decorative piping accents on the innovative Zoa™ bioleather seats

## Craftsmanship and Quality Assurance

Ensuring the recycled textile meets ultra-luxury standards is paramount. Ember Automotive collaborates with Tessitura Monti, a high-end Italian textile mill located near Como, Italy. Tessitura Monti weaves the Seaqual Yarn into bespoke fabrics tailored to Ember's specifications. These fabrics must meet stringent requirements for both durability, tested to withstand over 50,000 Martindale cycles (a measure of abrasion resistance), and the specific aesthetic vision set forth by co-founder Isabella Rossi.

## Traceability and Environmental Impact

Transparency and verification are key components of Ember's sustainable sourcing. Every shipment of Seaqual Yarn used by the company comes with a certification and a unique tracking number provided by the SEAQUAL INITIATIVE. This allows for the origin of the material from marine waste to be independently verified, a process audited annually by firms such as SCS Global Services.

By choosing Seaqual Yarn over conventional materials, Ember Automotive estimates a significant environmental benefit. The carbon footprint associated with these specific textile components is reduced by approximately 60% compared to using equivalent virgin polyester. Based on its target production volume of 500 vehicles annually, Ember's commitment translates to utilizing textiles derived from the equivalent of roughly 250,000 recycled ocean plastic bottles each year, actively contributing to cleaner oceans while delivering unparalleled luxury.
