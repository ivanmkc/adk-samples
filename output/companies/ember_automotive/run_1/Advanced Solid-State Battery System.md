# Ember Automotive's Advanced Solid-State Battery System

At the heart of Ember Automotive's ultra-luxury electric vehicles, the Aura sedan and Solstice SUV, lies a cutting-edge Advanced Solid-State Battery System. This proprietary technology is fundamental to achieving the exceptional range, rapid charging capabilities, and enhanced safety profile that define Ember vehicles.

## Energy Capacity and Vehicle Range

The battery system is tailored to meet the specific demands of each model. The Ember Aura sedan features a solid-state battery pack with a usable energy capacity of approximately 150 kWh, contributing significantly to its EPA-estimated range exceeding 550 miles. To power the larger Ember Solstice SUV and enable its estimated 520-mile range, a slightly larger pack with an estimated usable capacity of 155 kWh is employed.

## High-Voltage Architecture and Rapid Charging

Ember utilizes a sophisticated 800V class electrical architecture for its battery system. This high-voltage design allows for remarkable charging performance. Under optimal thermal conditions, the system is engineered to handle peak DC fast charging rates exceeding 400 kW. This capability enables charging from 10% to 80% state-of-charge in under 15 minutes when connected to a compatible high-power DC fast charger. The nominal voltage of the fully charged pack is approximately 750 Volts.

## Breakthrough Energy Density and Packaging

A key advantage of Ember's solid-state technology is its impressive energy density, which surpasses 450 Wh/kg. This high density allows the battery pack to be over 40% lighter than a conventional liquid-electrolyte lithium-ion battery pack offering the same energy capacity (comparing to typical Li-ion cells around 250 Wh/kg). This weight reduction contributes positively to vehicle dynamics and overall efficiency. The system utilizes large-format prismatic solid-state cells, a design choice optimizing energy packaging density and facilitating effective thermal management within the vehicle's structure.

## Enhanced Safety and Thermal Stability

Safety is paramount in Ember's battery design. The use of a solid electrolyte chemistry significantly enhances thermal stability compared to traditional lithium-ion batteries that use liquid electrolytes. This inherently reduces the risk of thermal runaway, a critical safety concern in battery technology. Complementing the intrinsic safety of the cells is Ember's proprietary liquid thermal management system. This active system circulates coolant through channels integrated within the battery modules, meticulously maintaining optimal cell temperatures between 20°C and 45°C, even during high-power operation and rapid charging events.

## Longevity and Warranty Assurance

Ember's solid-state battery pack is engineered for exceptional longevity. The company targets the retention of over 85% of the battery's initial energy capacity even after 2,000 full charge-discharge cycles. Underscoring this durability, Ember Automotive provides a specific warranty for the solid-state battery pack covering 8 years or 150,000 miles, whichever comes first. This warranty guarantees that the battery will retain at least 75% of its original usable capacity throughout the warranty period.

In summary, Ember Automotive's Advanced Solid-State Battery System represents a significant technological advancement, offering a compelling combination of high energy density, rapid charging, enhanced safety, and long operational life, underpinning the performance and luxury credentials of its electric vehicles.
